**Settings** › **People and access**: "Who can sign in to the box, what they see, and from where."

**When you need it:** a new colleague starts, someone leaves, a password or first sign-in link is lost, a person should see other products, or you want to reach the box from home.

**Who sees it:** administrators only. Adding a person, changing a name or e-mail, new passwords and links, and switching remote access on work in the office only: "For safety, passwords, keys and other secrets are entered only in the office."

This page, as described here, came with release 2026.10.25-af84ac2.

## People [#people-list]

**What it is for:** everyone who can sign in to this box. Each row shows the name, **Administrator** or **Member** with the e-mail, and, when something needs attention, one line such as "Has not signed in yet", "Has not signed in yet. The sign-in link has expired.", "The sign-in link was withdrawn", "Signs in next with a one-time password" or "Cannot sign in: switched off". Press a row to open that person's page.

A removed person stays in the list for 30 days: "removed on" a date, "Undo until" a date.

## Add a person [#invite]

**What it is for:** inviting a new colleague. "The box makes a one-time link for the first sign-in."

| Item | What it does | What happens when you use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Name**, **Work e-mail** | Who the person is. | |
| **Sees these products** | The products the person may see and ask about. All are ticked. | You can change it later on the person's page. |
| **Administrator, agents** | Folded: **Member: uses the agents** (the default) or **Administrator: sees every product and opens Settings**, and which agents the person may use. | For an administrator, enter **Your own password, to invite an administrator**. |
| **Add the person** | Makes the person and the one-time link. | The window shows the link once, with **Copy**, **Send from my e-mail** and **Copy the invitation**, then **Done**. The box sends no e-mail itself. |

More: [Members](/setup/members/).

## A person's page [#person]

Open a person from the list.

| Item | What it does | What happens when you use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sees these products** | "Tick what" the person "may see and ask about." Administrators see every product. | "Saved as you tick. Agents answer this person only from the products ticked here." |
| **Change name or e-mail** | Corrects the name or the work e-mail. | **Save** saves it. |
| **Forgotten password**: **Make a one-time password** | For a person who cannot sign in. | The box asks first: their current password stops working now. The window shows the one-time password once; with it the person chooses a new password. |
| **First sign-in**: **Make a new sign-in link** | For a person who has not used the first link. **Withdraw the link** takes it back. | "The link from before stops working now." For an administrator, the box asks for your own password first. |
| **Let** the person **sign in again** | Only for a person an older version of the box switched off. | They sign in with the password they had. |
| **Remove** the person **from the box** | Removes the person. | One confirmation lists what happens: they can no longer sign in; their mailbox and calendar connections stop at once; their private chats and what Truchsess learned about them are kept for 30 days so you can undo, then deleted; what they added to the company's products stays. **Undo** works for 30 days. |

Your own row links to **Change your own password in Your account**. Another administrator changes their own name, e-mail and password in **Your account**.

## Reach the box from home [#remote]

**What it is for:** signing in from home or on the road, with an e-mail code. Off: "The box opens only in your office network."

| Item | What it does | What happens when you use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Switch on** | Opens the steps: **Code from Truchsess support** (typed once, never shown again), **Settings can also be opened from home**, and **I have read what passes through Truchsess.** | **Switch on** switches it on. In the office only. **Who runs the connection**: Cloudflare, Inc., for Truchsess. |
| **Settings can also be opened from home** | Off: "from home, administrators see Home, Chat and Agents. Settings opens only in the office." | **Change** next to the line changes it later. |
| **Switch off** | Stops it. | The box asks first: "From home, nobody can sign in any more. In the office everything stays the same. Switching on again needs a new code from support." **Yes, switch off** does it. |

More: [Remote access](/admin/remote-access/).
