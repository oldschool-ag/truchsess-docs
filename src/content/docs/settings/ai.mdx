**Settings** › **The AI and costs**: "Which AI your agents use, and what it costs this month."

**When you need it:** to see this month's cost, to raise or lower the limit, after the box says the limit is reached, to ask for more credit, or to use your own AI key.

**Who sees it:** administrators only.

This page, as described here, came with release 2026.10.25-af84ac2.

## Your AI [#your-ai]

**What it is for:** which AI your agents use now, marked **In use**.

- **The AI that came with your box:** "It never keeps your data and never trains on it. The box picks the right model for each task."
- **Your own key**, with the AI company's name.
- While your ChatGPT plan is signed in, one line names its models: "An agent can run on it with" those models: "choose it in the agent's settings." See [Choose an agent's AI](/admin/agent-ai/).

| Item | What it does | What happens when you use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Use your own AI key** | Uses your own key at an AI company instead of the AI that came with the box. | Choose **Which AI company?**, paste the key, and press **Test and use this key**. Before the box uses it, it asks once what that AI company keeps and whether it trains on your data (the one data question). **Cancel** changes nothing. |
| **Use the AI that came with the box again** | Shown while you use your own key. | The box asks first: "Your agents use the AI that came with the box from their next task." **Yes, switch back** does it. |

With your own key, every amount on this page is marked **estimated**: your AI company bills you directly, and "your limit still stops your agents at this estimate."

## This month [#month]

**What it is for:** what the AI cost this month, per agent, against the one monthly limit.

| Item | What it does | What happens when you use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Amount and bar | What was used of the limit this month, and when it "starts again from zero". | Nothing changes. |
| One row per agent | Each agent that had costs this month, the most expensive first: its name, what it does and its cost. **Truchsess** is the answers in **Ask Truchsess**; **Chat** is "Questions you and your team asked". | An agent's row also says how many of its runs your ChatGPT plan carried. Those runs cost nothing on the box's budget. |
| **Agents you pay for** | Shown while you pay for extra copies of an agent: one line per copy, then the total. | Nothing changes. |

## Monthly limit [#limit]

**What it is for:** the most the AI may cost in a month. "Your agents stop when the limit is reached. The box warns you on Home at 80%."

| Item | What it does | What happens when you use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Monthly limit** | The new limit, in your currency. | **Save** saves it. When the limit is reached, the page says "Your agents stopped" and when they start again; raising the limit starts them again at once. |

More: [Budget](/admin/budget/).

## What happened [#events]

**What it is for:** this month's warnings and stops, newest first: "Most of the limit is used.", "Limit reached. Agents stopped.", "The AI key was refused. Agents stopped.", "The AI key works again.", "More credit added." Without any: "No warnings and no stops this month."

## More credit [#credit]

**What it is for:** "Credit for the AI that came with the box comes from Truchsess. Ask here; the answer shows on Home."

| Item | What it does | What happens when you use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ask for more credit** | Asks Truchsess for more credit: **How much?** and a **Note for Truchsess (optional)**. | **Send the request**. The request shows here with its state, for example "Waiting for Truchsess". "You pay only when they add the credit." |
