**Settings** › **The AI and costs**: "What the AI cost this month, and the most it may cost."

**When you need it:** to check this month's cost, to raise or lower the limit, or after the box says the limit is reached.

**Who sees it:** administrators only.

## This month [#month]

**What it is for:** how much of the month's limit the AI has used.

| Item | What it does | What happens when you use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **AI cost this month** | The amount, then "of" the limit "this month", with a bar. With a starter credit: "of the starter credit of" the amount, and "Starter credit left". | Nothing changes. The bar turns yellow near the limit and red at it. |
| **Agents you pay for** | Shown while you pay for extra copies of an agent: one line per copy, then the total. | Nothing changes. |
| Private mailboxes | When people read private mailboxes with agents: "Included in the AI use above." | Nothing changes. |

## Monthly limit [#limit]

**What it is for:** the most the AI may cost in a month.

| Item | What it does | What happens when you use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Monthly limit (CHF, whole amount)** | The new limit. | **Save** saves it: "Budget saved." The change is recorded in the activity record. With the AI that came with your box, the limit cannot go above what your key allows; a higher limit needs more credit from Truchsess. With a starter credit there is no monthly limit to change. |

More: [Budget](/admin/budget/) and [Budget at setup](/setup/budget/).

## Your AI and your data [#your-ai]

Two lines say which AI the box uses and what the AI company may do with your data:

- "AI: the AI that came with your box, chosen and paid through Truchsess." or "AI: your own AI key. The box counts what it costs".
- "Your data is never kept or used for training." or, when your data rule allows it, that the AI company may keep your data.

To change either, open [Advanced › AI key, data and costs](/settings/advanced/costs/). The lines still call that place "Settings > Advanced > AI".
