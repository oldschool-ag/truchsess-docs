**Your account** holds what belongs to you alone: light or dark, your password, signing out, your mailboxes and calendars, what Truchsess has learned from you, and your old assistant conversations.

**Where:** press your initials and name at the bottom of the menu (on a phone, the picture with your initials at the top right). The address is `https://myai.local/#account`.

**Who sees it:** everyone with an account, members and administrators. Each person sees only their own account. Administrators do not see other people's mailboxes, calendars or lessons.

This page, as described here, came with release 2026.10.25-af84ac2.

## You [#you]

### Light or dark [#theme]

| Item | What it does | What happens when you use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Like this device**, **Light**, **Dark** | **Like this device** "follows the light or dark setting of this phone or computer." | Changes at once, in this browser only. |

### Change your password [#password]

**When you need it:** your first sign-in with a one-time password, or when you think someone else knows it.

| Item | What it does | What happens when you use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Change your password** | Opens **Current password**, **New password** and **New password again**. The new password needs at least 12 characters, with upper- and lower-case letters and a number. | **Change password** saves it. "Your other signed-in browsers are signed out; this one stays signed in." **Cancel** changes nothing. |
| **Sign out** | Signs you out of this browser. | At once, without a question. On the remote address it also ends the e-mail code session. |

On the remote address the password form is not shown: "On the remote address you sign in with your e-mail code; your password is changed in the office, on the local address."

## Mail and calendars [#connections]

"Penn reads your mail and writes drafts. Penn never sends; you send from your own mail." Only your agents use your mailboxes and calendars, and only for your tasks. Connecting and disconnecting works in the office only.

If Penn is not added to the box yet, a card says so: you can connect your mailboxes and calendars now, and nothing is read until Penn is added under **Agents**.

### Mailboxes [#mail]

One row per mailbox, with its provider and its state.

| Item | What it does | What happens when you use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Add a mailbox** | Connects a company or private mailbox. | Type the address first. The box finds the way (Microsoft or Google sign-in, an app password with the steps, or the password) and asks only for that. For a Google Workspace company, **Copy the steps for your administrator** gives the steps as plain text. |
| Disconnect on a row | Removes the mailbox from the box. | The row says what was disconnected, with **Undo** for 8 seconds. After that the box deletes its access. Leaving the page disconnects at once. |

More: [Connect your company mailbox](/use/email/) and [Add a private mailbox](/use/private-mailbox/).

### Calendars [#calendars]

Home shows today's and tomorrow's meetings from your calendars, and an agent you give a task can see your meetings and when you are free. Agents only read.

| Item | What it does | What happens when you use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Add a calendar** | Connects a calendar: the choices your box offers. | Fill in what it asks and connect. |
| Disconnect on a row | Removes the calendar. | **Undo** for 8 seconds, as for a mailbox. |

More: [Connect your calendars](/use/calendars/).

## What Truchsess has learned [#learned]

"Only you see what Truchsess learns from you."

| Item | What it does | What happens when you use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Open** | Shows what waits for you, how each agent writes for you, your pages and everything in your memory. | Keep, change or delete each lesson there; **Undo** takes the last change back. |
| **Download** | Saves everything in your memory as one file. | Nothing changes. |

More: [What Truchsess has learned](/use/what-truchsess-learned/).

## Your old assistant conversations [#old-conversations]

Shown only when you wrote to the box's old built-in assistant. "Only you open or download them."

| Item | What it does | What happens when you use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Open your messages** | Shows them in a table. | **Close these messages** hides them again. |
| **Download as a file** | Saves them on your computer. | Nothing changes on the box. |
