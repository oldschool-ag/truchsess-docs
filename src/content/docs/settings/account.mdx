**Your account** holds what belongs to you alone: your password, light or dark, signing out, your own mailboxes and calendars, what Truchsess has learned from you, and your old assistant conversations.

**Where:** press your initials and name at the bottom of the menu (on a phone, the picture with your initials at the top right). The address is `https://myai.local/#account`.

**Who sees it:** everyone with an account, members and administrators. Each person sees only their own account. Administrators do not see other people's mailboxes, calendars or lessons.

At the top, one line says who you are: your name, your e-mail and your role (**Administrator** or **Member**).

## Light or dark [#theme]

**What it is for:** the look of the box on this computer.

| Item | What it does | What happens when you use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Light**, **Dark** | Shows the box in light or dark colours. | Changes at once, in this browser only. Until you pick one, the box follows your device's setting. |

## Change your password [#password]

**What it is for:** your own box password.

**When you need it:** your first sign-in with a one-time password, or when you think someone else knows it.

| Item | What it does | What happens when you use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Current password**, **New password**, **New password again** | The new password needs at least 12 characters, with upper- and lower-case letters and a number. | **Change password** saves it: "Password changed. Your other signed-in browsers are signed out." This browser stays signed in. If the two new passwords differ, the box says so and changes nothing. |
| **Sign out** | Signs you out of this browser. | At once, without a question. On the remote address it also ends the e-mail code session. |

On the remote address the password form is not shown: "On the remote address you sign in with your e-mail code; your password is changed in the office, on the local address."

## Your connections [#connections]

"Your own mailbox and calendars. Only your agents use them, and only for your tasks." Connecting and disconnecting works in the office only.

### Email inbox [#mail]

**What it is for:** your company mailbox, and one private mailbox if you like. "Agents you give a task can read your mail and write drafts into your Drafts folder. They never send: you read a draft and send it yourself."

**When you need it:** before you ask an agent to answer mail for you.

| Item | What it does | What happens when you use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Add a company mailbox** | Connects your mailbox at the company's mail domain. | Enter your **Email address** and password (or sign in with Microsoft or Google), then **Test and connect**. The box tests it first: "connected" when it worked. If the administrator has not set the company's mail domains yet, the box says so and you cannot add one. |
| **Add a private mailbox (only you)** | Connects one private mailbox. Its card says **Private: only you**. | Only your agents read it, for your tasks. |
| **Open your drafts** | Opens the drafts agents wrote for you. | Nothing changes. |
| **Connect again** | Connects a mailbox that says **Not working** or **Needs to be connected again**. | The mailbox works again with its new password or sign-in. |
| **Disconnect** | Removes the mailbox from the box. | Press **Confirm disconnect** within 10 seconds. Agents can no longer use it, and the box deletes its password or access at once. Connect it again to undo. |
| Company signature | Shares the closing lines of your last 5 sent mails, so drafts end like your own mails. | **Yes, share the closing lines of my last 5 sent mails** starts it; **Stop sharing my signature** stops it. |

More: [Connect your company mailbox](/use/email/) and [Add a private mailbox](/use/private-mailbox/).

### Calendars [#calendars]

**What it is for:** "Home shows today's and tomorrow's meetings from all of them, and an agent you give a task can see your meetings and when you are free. Agents only read: they never create, change, accept, decline or delete anything. Only you see your calendars."

| Item | What it does | What happens when you use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Add calendar** | Connects a calendar: the choices your box offers, for example **Apple iCloud** or **Another calendar (for example Hostpoint)**. Up to 10. | Fill in the fields and press **Test and connect**. A choice your administrator has not set up yet says so. |
| **Show on Home and to your agents** | Ticks per calendar. | **Save** keeps the choice. |
| **Connect again**, **Disconnect** | Repairs or removes a connection. | **Disconnect** asks first: Home and your agents no longer see these calendars, and the box deletes its access at once. |

More: [Connect your calendars](/use/calendars/).

## What Truchsess has learned [#learned]

**What it is for:** "What each agent learned from you: how you change the drafts it writes, what you sent back, declined or hid with a reason, and what you asked it to remember. Only you see your lessons; administrators do not. A lesson applies at once; Undo takes the last change back."

| Item | What it does | What happens when you use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Waiting for you** | What an agent proposes to remember. "Nothing here is used until you keep it." | **Keep** uses it from now on; **Delete** drops it. |
| A lesson | One thing an agent learned, under that agent. | **Edit** then **Save** changes it, **Delete** removes it, **Undo** takes the last change back. |
| **Everything in your memory** | Every record the box keeps for you. | **Download** saves all of it as one file. **Edit**, **Delete** and **Undo** work per record. |
| **Company mail standard** | "Set by the administrator; every mail draft follows it." | You read it here; the administrator changes it. |
| **Numbers for experts** | Your edits, and your tasks sent back or declined, per week. | Nothing changes. |

More: [What Truchsess has learned](/use/what-truchsess-learned/).

## Your old assistant conversations [#old-conversations]

**What it is for:** shown only when you wrote to the box's old assistant. "You wrote" so many messages, and when the last was.

| Item | What it does | What happens when you use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Open your messages** | Shows them in a table. | **Close these messages** hides them again. |
| **Download as a file** | Saves them on your computer. | Nothing changes on the box. |
