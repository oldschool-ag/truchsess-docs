**Settings** › **Advanced** › **Product access**: who sees which product. It opens its one part directly.

**When you need it:** a member should work on a product, or should no longer see it. "Members cannot see product agents until this assignment exists."

**Who sees it:** administrators only.

Its card says how product access stands: "Everyone sees every product" (on a new box), how many people see the products assigned to them, or "Members see a product only once you assign it".

This topic came with release 2026.10.25-3161da0.

## Product access [#product-access]

| Item | What it does | What happens when you use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **User**, **Product**, **Access** | Who, which product, and as **Member** or **Owner**. | **Assign access** saves it at once: "Product access assigned." |
| **Remove** on an assignment | Takes the access away. | Press **Confirm remove**: "Product access removed." Assign it again to undo. |
