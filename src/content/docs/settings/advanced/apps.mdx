**Settings** › **Advanced** › **Your own apps at Microsoft, Google and GitHub**: "Only if your IT wants its own registration. Without it, the Truchsess apps are used."

**Who sees it:** administrators only. Every change here works in the office only. After a save, the box shows the steps it works through.

<ComingNext>
This topic comes with the next update. In release 2026.10.24-2bb4bff the apps are cards in the **Company mail** panel under **Advanced** › **Connections**.
</ComingNext>

## Microsoft 365 [#microsoft]

**What it is for:** mail through your own app at Microsoft. Without it: "Not set up: mail uses the Truchsess app at Microsoft."

| Item | What it does | What happens when you use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Directory (tenant) ID**, **Application (client) ID**, **Client secret**, **Secret expires on** | Your app's details from Microsoft Entra. The box keeps no copy of the secret in the page. | **Save** (or **Replace**) saves them. The line then says the tenant and when the secret expires. Shortly before it expires, the part says so: make a new secret in Microsoft Entra and enter it here. |
| **Remove** | Removes your app. | The box asks first: "Every mailbox connected through it is disconnected." |
| **How to set this up** | Seven steps in Microsoft Entra, and the redirect address to enter exactly. | Nothing changes. |
| **Another Truchsess app ID** | Only if Truchsess support gave you another one. | **Save** saves it; an empty field goes back to the usual one. |

## Google [#google]

**What it is for:** "Google Workspace needs your own app: your IT makes it once in the Google admin console."

| Item | What it does | What happens when you use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Client ID**, **Client secret** | Your app's details from Google. | **Save** (or **Replace**) saves them. **Remove** asks first, as for Microsoft. |
| **How to set this up** | Seven steps in the Google console, and the redirect address. | Nothing changes. |

## GitHub [#github]

**What it is for:** "Use an existing GitHub App instead of the Truchsess one."

| Item | What it does | What happens when you use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **GitHub organisation**, **App ID**, **Installation ID**, **Private key (.pem)** | Your app's details from GitHub. | **Use this GitHub App** stores them; the form empties. The change is recorded in the activity record. |

More: [Connections](/admin/connections/).

## Mailboxes connected through these apps [#mailboxes]

**What it is for:** which people's mailboxes run through your apps. It shows only when there is one.

| Item | What it does | What happens when you use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A mailbox | The person's name (never the address), the provider and the state. | **Remove** asks first, then removes that mailbox from the box. Its owner connects it again in Your account. |
