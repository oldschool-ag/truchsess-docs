**Settings** › **Advanced (for experts)**: "For developers, IT and support. You do not need it to set up or run the box."

**Who sees it:** administrators only. A member who opens an Advanced address sees "Only for administrators".

Advanced as cards came with release 2026.10.25-3161da0. Links to the earlier Advanced parts still open the new place (see below).

## How Advanced works [#cards]

Advanced opens with one card per topic. Each card shows the topic's name and one line on how it stands. Press a card to open the topic: its parts are cards too. A part opens on its own page, with **Back to** the topic and the path at the top: Settings › Advanced › topic › part. A topic with only one part opens that part directly.

Every page of Advanced has its own address, for example `https://myai.local/#advanced/support/ssh`. You can reload it, bookmark it, open it in a new tab or send it to a colleague.

On a computer, the left menu lists Settings' pages beneath **Settings**, and Advanced's topics beneath **Advanced**. On a phone there is no side menu; the cards are the list.
