**Settings** › **Advanced** › **Older data**: "Bring in data from an older Truchsess box, and see old assistant conversations."

**When you need it:** once, when you move from an older Truchsess box, or to clean up conversations with the assistant the box had before agents.

**Who sees it:** administrators only. Importing and deleting work in the office only.

Its card says how many people's old conversations are kept, for example "Old conversations of 2 people are kept".

## Import from an older box [#import]

**What it is for:** bringing the memory of an older Truchsess box into this one.

| Item | What it does | What happens when you use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The export (.zip)** and **Upload and review** | Uploads the export the older box made and lists what it holds. | Nothing is imported yet. The summary says how many items are new, changed or already here, and how many the safety scan blocked. |
| Filters | **Kind**, **State**, **Scan**, **Folder** and **Find** narrow the list. | |
| A row's place and tick | Where the item goes, and whether it is imported. | Saved at once. **Tick all shown**, **Untick all shown** and **Set place** work on all rows shown. |
| **Import selected** | Imports the ticked items. | "You can leave the page; the import goes on." Every imported record counts as unconfirmed until a person confirms it; checks wait on Home. An import cannot be undone. If it stops, what was written stays; upload the zip again for the rest. |
| **Discard** | Drops the upload. | Nothing is imported. |

## Old assistant conversations [#old-conversations]

**What it is for:** "From the assistant before agents. Only you see your own; administrators see who wrote them." "Nobody opens another person's messages, administrators included."

One row per person: the name, how many conversations, and until when.

| Item | What it does | What happens when you use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Open your messages**, **Download as a file** | Only on your own row. | Shows or saves your own messages. Nothing changes. |
| **Delete these conversations** | Deletes one person's old conversations. | The box asks first: "Undo brings them back within 15 minutes." After that they are gone for good. |

Each person also finds their own old conversations in [Your account](/settings/account/#old-conversations).
