## Who can do this

The administrator, once, on a new box.

## Before you start

- The box is installed. See [Unbox and connect](/setup/unbox-and-connect/).
- The **CEO bootstrap code** from the end of the installation.
- A password for yourself: at least 12 characters, with upper-case and lower-case letters and a number.

## Steps

1. Open **https://myai.local/** in your browser.
2. The page **Create the CEO administrator** opens.
3. Enter your name under **CEO name**.
4. Enter your e-mail address under **Work email**. You sign in with it.
5. Enter your **Password**.
6. Enter the code under **Bootstrap code**.
7. Press **Create administrator**.

## What you see afterwards

- You are signed in. The header shows your name and "CEO administrator".
- The box asks you at once to choose its AI: the page **Choose the appliance AI**. Continue with [AI providers](/setup/ai-providers/).
- The bootstrap code is used up. It cannot create a second account.

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Create administrator** | Creates the one administrator account of the box. | A new box for a new start. | No. The account cannot be removed or replaced in the portal. |
| **Restore from a backup** (link under the form) | Brings back the users, workers, tasks and settings of another box from a backup file. You sign in with the accounts from the backup. | You move to a new box, or you replace a broken box. See [Backup and restore](/admin/backup-and-restore/#restore-on-a-new-box). | No, once started. |

## If something goes wrong

- "The bootstrap code is missing or wrong.": check the code. Letters are upper-case. The letters O and I and the numbers 0 and 1 are never used.
- "Too many login attempts. Wait 15 minutes and try again.": wait 15 minutes.
- Lost the code: on the box's console (screen and keyboard), the command shown in the note under the form prints it again, as long as no account exists. Ask support if you need help.

<NotYet>
You cannot change the administrator's e-mail address or reset a forgotten administrator password in the portal. Keep the password in a password manager. If it is lost, ask support.
</NotYet>
