## Who can do this

The administrator.

## What you need

- The AI provider is set. See [AI providers](/setup/ai-providers/).
- Know your monthly amount. With **Managed by Old School**, your metered key has a limit from the vendor. Your budget cannot be higher than that limit.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings** › **Plan and costs**.
2. Enter the **Monthly limit** as a whole amount.
3. Press **Save**.

The box decides the warning and stop levels: a warning at 80 percent, new tasks stop at 100 percent. You do not need to change them.

### Change the warning and stop levels (experts)

1. Open **Settings** › **Advanced** › **AI**, panel **Costs in detail**. **Advanced** is the link **Advanced (for experts)** at the bottom of **Settings**.
2. Under **Edit the budget**, enter **Warn at (percent)**. The default is 80.
3. Enter **Stop new runs at (percent)**. The default is 100.
4. Enter **Period starts on day** (1 to 28). The default is 1.
5. Leave **Billing rate** as it is unless the vendor gave you another rate.
6. Press **Save budget**.

## What you see afterwards

- **Plan and costs** shows **AI cost this month**, the amount and a bar of how much of the monthly limit is used. Below it: "Your data is never kept or used for training." and which AI the box uses, in one line.
- **Costs in detail** shows **Monthly limit**, **Used this period**, **Remaining** and **Projected for the period**.
- When usage reaches the warn level, everyone sees a yellow banner above the chat. Tasks still start.
- When usage reaches the stop level, the banner turns red and new tasks do not start. Tasks already running finish their current step.

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Monthly limit** | The most the box may spend per period. With **Managed by Old School**, 0 means "the key's limit is the budget". | Always set it. | Yes. |
| **Warn at (percent)** | When the yellow warning starts. | Lower it for an earlier warning. | Yes. |
| **Stop new runs at (percent)** | When new tasks stop. | Keep 100 unless you want a safety margin. | Yes. |
| **Period starts on day** | The day the budget period begins each month. | Match your billing month. | Yes. |
| **Billing rate** | How the provider's currency is converted to yours. | Only when the vendor tells you. | Yes. |

With **Own key**, nothing is enforced: "In own-key mode the limit is informational; switch to managed AI with an activation code to enforce it." The box counts what your own key costs and shows it.

## With the starter credit

A box that is switched to the managed AI with an activation code gets a one-time starter credit instead of a monthly budget. Its amount comes from the store; the first setup shows it. You do not set a limit for it.

- **Plan and costs** shows the amount used "of the starter credit of ..." and "Starter credit left: ...". There is no monthly reset.
- **Costs in detail** shows **Starter credit** and **Used since activation**.
- At 80 percent everyone sees a warning banner. At 100 percent the banner says: "The AI starter credit is used up. New runs are paused. Request a top-up, or use your own AI key." The administrator sees the buttons **Request top-up** and **Use my own AI key**.
- A new monthly limit is refused while the box runs on the starter credit: "The starter credit has no monthly limit to change. When it is used up, request a top-up or use your own AI key."

Details: [Budget](/admin/budget/#the-starter-credit).

The starter credit comes with the activation in the first setup. With your own AI key there is none: set a monthly limit as above.
