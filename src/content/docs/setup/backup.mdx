The box makes no backup until a recovery key exists. Do this step now.

## Who can do this

The administrator, in the office on **https://myai.local/**.

## Before you start

- A safe place for the recovery key that is not the box: a password manager, or a printout in a locked place.

## Steps

1. Open **Administration**, then **Backups**, panel **Backups**.
2. Under **Recovery key**, press **Create the recovery key**.
3. The key appears with the warning "This is the only time the box shows this key."
4. Press **Download as a file**, or **Copy** and paste it into your password manager.
5. Press **I have saved it**.
6. Press **Back up now** to make the first backup.
7. When it is done, press **Download** next to the backup in **Backups on this box** and keep the file somewhere other than the box.

<Aside type="danger" title="Keep the recovery key">
Every backup can only be opened with its recovery key. If the key is lost, those backups are lost too. The box keeps only a fingerprint of the key, not the key itself.
</Aside>

## What you see afterwards

- The panel says "Recovery key ..." with its fingerprint and when it was created.
- Every night at 03:30 (box time) the box makes a backup and keeps the newest 7.
- **Administration** › **Overview** no longer lists "There is no recovery key yet, so the box cannot make backups. Create it on Backups." under **Needs your attention**.
- "Backups on this box" lists each backup with **Made**, **Size**, **How** and **Recovery key**.

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Create the recovery key** | Starts the nightly backups. | Now. | n/a |
| **Create a new recovery key** | Replaces the key. Older backups then need the older key. | Only if the key may be known to someone else. Keep the old key as long as you keep its backups. | No. |
| **Back up now** | Makes a backup at once. | Before big changes, for example an update. | n/a |
| **Download** | Saves a backup file (`.age`, encrypted) to your computer. | Regularly, so a copy exists outside the box. | n/a |

What is not in a backup: secrets. These are the AI provider key, connection keys, the store enrolment and the remote access code. After a restore, the box lists what to enter again.

<NotYet>
You cannot choose the backup time or the number of backups kept, and the box cannot copy backups to another place by itself. Download a backup regularly and keep it safe.
</NotYet>
