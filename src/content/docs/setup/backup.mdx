Your recovery sheet holds the key that opens every safety copy of your box. If the box ever breaks or is lost, the sheet and a safety copy bring your company's data back onto a new box. Only you have the key: the box keeps only a fingerprint of it, and the publisher has nothing.

The box makes no safety copy until a recovery sheet exists. In the guided first setup you made it in step 3, **Your recovery sheet**, and the box made its first safety copy right after. Then only check it here and keep a copy outside the box. A box set up without the guided setup needs the sheet now: see [Make the recovery sheet](#make-the-recovery-sheet).

## Who can do this

The administrator, in the office, at the box's own address.

## What you need

- A safe place for the recovery sheet that is not the box: a password manager, or the printout in a locked place.
- For a copy outside the box: a computer or a drive where you keep files safe.
- To move to a new box: the new box with its card, a safety copy file of the old box (a file ending in `.age`), and the recovery sheet of that copy.

## Steps

### Check the sheet from the first setup

1. Open **Settings** › **Updates and safety copies**.
2. Under **Recovery sheet**, read the line "Recovery sheet made ..." with the date and who made it.
3. Under **Copies on this box**, find the first safety copy.

### Make the recovery sheet

Only if the box has no recovery sheet yet. **Settings** › **Updates and safety copies** then says "No recovery sheet yet: the box makes no safety copy until it exists."

1. Open **Settings** › **Updates and safety copies**.
2. Under **Recovery sheet**, press **Make the recovery sheet**.
3. The key appears with the warning "The box shows this only once. Save it now, away from the box (a password manager, or printed and locked away). Every copy from now on opens only with it."
4. Press **Download as a file**, or **Copy** and paste it into your password manager.
5. Press **I have saved it**.
6. Press **Back up now** to make the first safety copy.

### Keep a copy outside the box

Until your storage in France is set up, the safety copies stay on the box. If the box breaks, they break with it. Once the storage is set up, the box also sends each night's copy to France by itself, see [The copy in France](/admin/backup-and-restore/#the-copy-in-france). Until then, keep a copy elsewhere:

1. Open **Settings** › **Updates and safety copies**.
2. Under **Copies on this box**, press **Download** next to the newest copy.
3. Keep the file somewhere other than the box, apart from the recovery sheet.

Do this regularly, for example once a week, and before big changes.

### Move to a new box

This is "I am moving from another Truchsess box". It brings the people, agents, tasks and settings of the old box onto a new box, from a safety copy file and its recovery sheet.

1. Connect the new box and open the address on its card. See [Unbox and connect](/setup/unbox-and-connect/).
2. On **Welcome to your Truchsess box**, press **I am moving from another Truchsess box**. Do not press **Set up a new box**: the restore works only before the first account exists.
3. The page **Restore from a backup** opens.
4. Enter the code from the new box's card under **Code from the card**. On a box installed from the USB stick, it is **Code from the installer**.
5. Under **Backup file**, choose the safety copy file of the old box.
6. Type the key from your recovery sheet under **Recovery key**. Or choose the file `truchsess-recovery-sheet.txt` under **or the recovery key file**; the box reads the key from it as it is.
7. Press **Restore**.
8. Wait while the page ticks off the steps of the restore. The box restarts its programs; the page reconnects by itself.
9. Read the table **Enter these again**: "Secrets are never in a backup. Sign in with an account from the backup, then enter each of these where it says."
10. Press **Sign in** and sign in with an account from the old box.
11. Enter each item of the table where it says. For the AI, you can use the code from the new box's card under **Settings** › **Advanced** › **AI**, panel **AI of this box**, **Switch to managed AI with an activation code**. See [AI providers and lanes](/admin/ai-providers-and-lanes/#switch-to-managed-ai-with-an-activation-code). **Advanced** is the link **Advanced (for experts)** at the bottom of **Settings**.

The same steps are on the recovery sheet itself, under "How to bring the data back".

<Aside type="danger" title={'Keep the recovery key'}>
Every safety copy can only be opened with the key on its recovery sheet. If the key is lost, those copies are lost too. Anyone with the sheet and a safety copy of the box can read your company's data: keep the two apart, and away from the box.
</Aside>

## What you see afterwards

- **Settings** › **Updates and safety copies** says "Recovery sheet made ..." with the date and who made it.
- Every night at 03:30, in your company's time zone, the box makes a safety copy and keeps the last 7.
- **Home** no longer says "No safety copy yet".
- **Copies on this box** lists each copy with **Made**, **Size**, **How** and **Recovery sheet**.
- After a move: the new box has the people, agents, tasks and settings of the copy. Everyone signs in with their accounts from the old box.

What is not in a safety copy: passwords and keys. These are the AI provider key, connection keys, the store enrolment and the remote access code. After a restore, the box lists what to enter again under **Enter these again**.

## If it does not work

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "No recovery sheet yet: the box makes no safety copy until it exists." | Make it now. See [Make the recovery sheet](#make-the-recovery-sheet). |
| The page asks for the code, and you do not have the new box's card. | The restore needs the code of the new box, not of the old one. Find the card that came with the new box, or ask support. |
| "That is not a recovery key. It is the line that starts with AGE-SECRET-KEY-1, from the file you saved when the key was created." | Type the key exactly as it is on the sheet, or choose the sheet's file. |
| "This recovery key does not open this backup. Use the recovery key that was current when the backup was made. Nothing was changed." | Use the sheet that was current when the copy was made. A copy made before a newer sheet opens only with the older sheet. |
| "Choose the backup file first." | Choose the safety copy file under **Backup file**. |
| "This box is already set up: sign in and restore under Settings > Safety copies." | The new box already has an account. Restore under **Settings** › **Updates and safety copies**, section **Restore**. See [Safety copies and restore](/admin/backup-and-restore/). |
| You have no safety copy file of the old box, and the old box is broken. | The data cannot come back. On the new box, press **Set up a new box**. From now on, download a copy regularly. |

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Print the sheet or save it as a file | Paper goes in a locked place, a file in a password manager. Both work for a restore. | Whichever you keep safe more easily. | n/a |
| **Make the recovery sheet** | Starts the nightly safety copies. | Only if no sheet exists yet. | n/a |
| **Make a new recovery sheet** | Replaces the key. Older copies then need the older key. | Only if the key may be known to someone else. Keep the old sheet as long as you keep its copies. | No. |
| **Back up now** | Makes a safety copy at once. | Before big changes, for example an update. | n/a |
| **Download** | Saves a safety copy file (`.age`, encrypted) to your computer. | Regularly, so a copy exists outside the box. | n/a |
| **I am moving from another Truchsess box** | Restores the old box's work onto a new box. | A new or replacement box, before its first account. | No, once started. |
