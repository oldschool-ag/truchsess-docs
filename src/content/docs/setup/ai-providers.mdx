The box does not contain an AI model. It sends each task to an AI provider. A box activated with the activation code already uses the managed AI (see [First run](/setup/first-run/#activate-this-box)); then you can skip this step. This step connects the box to a provider you choose, with your own key.

## Who can do this

The administrator.

## What you need

Decide two things:

1. **Who pays the AI provider.**
   - **Own key**: your company has an account with an AI company and a key from it (a secret code from its website). You pay that company directly.
   - **Managed by Old School**: the vendor of the box issued a metered key for your box, with a monthly limit. You received this key from the vendor. (With an activation code the managed key arrives by itself; you do not paste it.)
2. **Which provider.** If your data may only be processed in Switzerland, choose **PHOENIQS (Switzerland)**. See [Swiss-only](/admin/ai-providers-and-lanes/#the-swiss-only-option).

## Steps

1. At the first run, press **Use my own AI key instead** on **Activate this box**: the page **The AI for your box** opens. Later you find it under **Settings** › **Advanced** › **AI**, panel **Own AI key**, button **Change AI provider**. **Advanced** is the link **Advanced (for experts)** at the bottom of **Settings**.
2. Choose **How you pay**: **Own key** or **Managed by Old School**.
3. Choose the **AI company**. With **Managed by Old School** it is fixed to OpenRouter.
4. Check the **AI model**. The box suggests a default model for the AI company. Keep it unless you know you need another.
5. Choose the **Kind of key** if there is more than one.
6. Paste the key into the key field (its label names the key, for example "OpenAI key").
7. With **Managed by Old School** you may enter the **Name of the key from Old School (optional)**.
8. Press **Save**.

Opened from **Settings**, the page keeps the menu on the left. **Back to Settings** at the top left returns to **Settings** › **Advanced** › **AI**, and **Cancel** returns to the page you came from. Neither saves anything. At the first run, **Cancel** returns to **Activate this box**.

## What you see afterwards

- The page **Starting the assistant** appears: "This page will continue automatically."
- Then the portal opens on **Home**. The status line at the bottom of the menu shows the provider and the model.
- The box has filled the three [model lanes](/start/glossary/#model-lane) with the provider's default models. You can change them later.
- The key stays on the box. The portal never shows it again.

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Own key** | "You use your own key and pay your AI company directly. Old School does not count or charge this use". **Settings** › **Plan and costs** shows what the box used, for you only. | You already pay a provider. | Yes, change it any time. |
| **Managed by Old School** | Uses the key Old School gave this box, through OpenRouter, with a monthly limit. You get a warning near the limit; at the limit new tasks wait until money is added. See [budget](/start/glossary/#budget). | You want one bill from the vendor and a hard monthly limit. | Yes. |
| OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, xAI / Grok, OpenRouter, Kimi / Moonshot | Well-known AI companies. You need a key from the company. | You have an account there. | Yes. |
| **PHOENIQS (Switzerland)** | Open models hosted only in Switzerland. The provider states it never keeps your data and never trains on it. Needs a PHOENIQS key. The box does not meter it. | Your data must stay in Switzerland. | Yes. |
| **Local AI / Ollama** | An AI server in your own network. | You run your own model server. | Yes. |
| **Your own AI server** | Any AI service with a compatible interface. You enter **Name of the AI service**, **Server address** and **Server type**. | Your IT runs its own AI service. | Yes. |

Changing the provider later rewrites the model lanes with the new provider's defaults. Then press **Apply to installed agents** under **Settings** › **Advanced** › **AI**, panel **Model lanes**, so that installed agents use the new models. See [AI providers and lanes](/admin/ai-providers-and-lanes/).
