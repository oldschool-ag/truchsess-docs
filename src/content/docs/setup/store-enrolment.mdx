The store is the publisher's catalog of agents for your box. You enrol the box once. Then you can subscribe to functions and install their agents.

If you activated the box at the first run with the activation code, the box is already enrolled: the activation code is the enrolment code. Skip to [After enrolling](#after-enrolling). See also [First run](/setup/first-run/#activate-this-box).

## Who can do this

The administrator, in the office on **https://truchsess.local/**.

## Before you start

You need from the publisher:

- the **store address**;
- a one-time **enrolment code**. It looks like `TRS-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX`;
- the publisher's public key: a `.pub` file. You need it if the Store says the publisher is "NOT trusted here yet".

How buying works is explained in the store's own documentation: [How buying works](https://faivr.ai/docs/how-buying-works).

## Steps

### Enrol

1. Open **Agents**.
2. Find the panel **Store**. It says "This appliance is not enrolled with a store yet."
3. Enter the **Store address**.
4. Enter the **Enrolment code**.
5. Optional: enter a **Label for this appliance**, for example your company name.
6. Press **Enrol**.
7. Wait until the steps are done and the panel says "Enrolled as ...".

### After enrolling

1. The Store may show nothing yet: "No catalog fetched yet. Press "Refresh catalog"." Press **Refresh catalog**.
2. Read the line about publisher keys under the enrolment details: "Publisher key the store offered: ...". Each key says "trusted here" or "NOT trusted here yet: register it under Trusted publishers".
3. If a key says "NOT trusted here yet", trust that publisher. Follow the next steps.

### Trust the store's publisher

1. Open **Administration** › **Agent rules**, panel **Trusted publishers**.
2. Paste the publisher's public key (the PEM text from its `.pub` file) under **Public key (PEM, from the .pub file)**. Or choose the file under **Or upload the .pub file**.
3. Enter a **Label**, for example the publisher's name.
4. Press **Add publisher**.
5. Go back to **Agents**, **Store** and press **Refresh catalog**. The key now says "trusted here".

A one-click trust button on the store card is coming with a later update. Until then, paste the key as above. More: [Trusted publishers](/admin/packages/#trusted-publishers).

## What you see afterwards

- The panel shows **Enrolled as**, **Store**, **Label**, **Enrolled**, **Last contact** and **Appliance key id**.
- After **Refresh catalog**, the Store lists the functions the store offers you, with their monthly price and their agents.
- The enrolment code is used once and is not kept on the box.

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Enrol** | Connects this box to the store. | Once. | No, not in the portal. |
| **Private test server (advanced)** | A certificate for a private test store. | Only for a test store, when the publisher asks for it. | n/a |
| **Refresh catalog** | Fetches the store's current catalog. | After enrolling, and after you trusted a publisher. | n/a |
| **Add publisher** | Trusts the publisher's signature. | When the Store says "NOT trusted here yet". | Yes: **Remove** under **Trusted publishers**. |

What leaves the box: the box's identity, which agents it asks for, and receipts for installs and uninstalls. Your tasks, files, knowledge and keys never go to the store.

Paying and cancelling: see [Billing](https://faivr.ai/docs/billing) and [Cancel](https://faivr.ai/docs/cancel) in the store's documentation.
