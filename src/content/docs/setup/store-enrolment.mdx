The store is the publisher's catalog of workers for your box. You enrol the box once. Then you can subscribe to functions and install their workers.

## Who can do this

The administrator, in the office on **https://myai.local/**.

## Before you start

You need two things from the publisher:

- the **store address**;
- a one-time **enrolment code**. It looks like `TRS-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX`.

How buying works is explained in the store's own documentation: [How buying works](https://faivr.ai/docs/how-buying-works).

## Steps

1. Open **Administration**, sub-tab **Packages**.
2. The first panel is **Store**. It says "This appliance is not enrolled with a store yet."
3. Enter the **Store address**.
4. Enter the **Enrolment code**.
5. Optional: enter a **Label for this appliance**, for example your company name.
6. Press **Enrol**.
7. Wait until the steps are done and the panel says "Enrolled as ...".
8. Look at the line about publisher keys. If it says "NOT trusted here yet", register that publisher. See [Trusted publishers](/admin/packages/#trusted-publishers).
9. Press **Refresh catalog**.

## What you see afterwards

- The panel shows **Enrolled as**, **Store**, **Label**, **Enrolled**, **Last contact** and **Appliance key id**.
- After **Refresh catalog**, the panel lists the functions the store offers you, with their monthly price and their workers.
- The enrolment code is used once and is not kept on the box.

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Enrol** | Connects this box to the store. | Once. | No, not in the portal. |
| **Private test server (advanced)** | A certificate for a private test store. | Only when the publisher asks for it. | n/a |

What leaves the box: the box's identity, which workers it asks for, and receipts for installs and uninstalls. Your tasks, files, knowledge and keys never go to the store.

Paying and cancelling: see [Billing](https://faivr.ai/docs/billing) and [Cancel](https://faivr.ai/docs/cancel) in the store's documentation.

<NotYet>
You cannot undo the enrolment in the portal. To stop paying, cancel each subscription. See [Store](/admin/store/#cancel-a-subscription).
</NotYet>
