The store is the publisher's catalog of agents for your box. You enrol the box once. Then you can subscribe to functions and install their agents.

If you activated the box at the first run with the activation code, the box is already enrolled: the activation code is the enrolment code. Skip to [After enrolling](#after-enrolling). See also [First run](/setup/first-run/#activate-this-box).

## Who can do this

The administrator, in the office on **https://truchsess.local/**.

## What you need

You need from the publisher:

- the **store address**, if the box does not fill it in by itself;
- a one-time **enrolment code**. It looks like `TRS-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX`.

How buying works is explained in the store's own documentation: [How buying works](https://faivr.ai/docs/how-buying-works).

## Steps

### Enrol

1. Open **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Agents**, panel **Store connection**. **Advanced** is the link **Advanced (for experts)** at the bottom of **Settings**. On **Agents**, the button **Connect to the store** in the **Store** opens this panel too.
2. The panel says "This box is not connected to a store yet. Enter the one-time code the publisher gave you."
3. Check the **Store address**. Enter it if it is empty.
4. Enter the **Enrolment code**.
5. Optional: enter a **Label for this appliance**, for example your company name.
6. Press **Enrol**.
7. Wait until the steps are done and the panel says "Connected to the store on ...".

### After enrolling

1. The box loads the store's catalog by itself. To load it again later, press **Refresh catalog** in the panel **Store connection**.
2. Open **Agents**. Above the **Store**, the box may ask: "The store's agents come from ... Allow agents from ... on this box so you can add them."
3. If it asks, trust that publisher. Follow the next steps.

### Trust the store's publisher

1. On **Agents**, above the **Store**, press **Allow agents from** and the publisher's name.
2. The question disappears, and the **Store** lists the publisher's agents.

The panel **Store connection** says the same: "... is a trusted publisher on this box." To undo it, remove the publisher under **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Agents**, panel **Trusted publishers**. That panel also takes a publisher's public key from a `.pub` file, for agents from outside the store. More: [Trusted publishers](/admin/packages/#trusted-publishers).

## What you see afterwards

- The panel **Store connection** says "Connected to the store on ..." and the last contact. **Details** shows **Store**, **Name of this box at the store**, **Box id at the store** and **Key of this box**.
- On **Agents**, the **Store** lists the functions the store offers you, with their monthly price and their agents.
- The enrolment code is used once and is not kept on the box.

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Enrol** | Connects this box to the store. | Once. | No, not in the portal. |
| **Private test server (advanced)** | A certificate for a private test store. Shown only when support switched it on for your box. | Only for a test store, when the publisher asks for it. | n/a |
| **Refresh catalog** | Fetches the store's current catalog. | When the store offers something new. | n/a |
| **Allow agents from** the publisher | Trusts the publisher's signature. | When **Agents** asks for it above the **Store**. | Yes: **Remove** under **Trusted publishers**. |

What leaves the box: the box's identity, which agents it asks for, and receipts for installs and uninstalls. Your tasks, files, knowledge and keys never go to the store.

Paying and cancelling: see [Billing](https://faivr.ai/docs/billing) and [Cancel](https://faivr.ai/docs/cancel) in the store's documentation.

<NotYet>
You cannot undo the enrolment in the portal. To stop paying, cancel each subscription. See [Store](/admin/store/#cancel-a-subscription).
</NotYet>
