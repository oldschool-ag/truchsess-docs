## Who can do this

The person who will be the administrator of the box.

## Before you start

- The box (a small computer) and its power supply.
- The Truchsess USB installer stick.
- A network cable to your office network. This is the easiest way. Wi-Fi also works.
- A laptop or phone with a web browser on the same office network.
- Recommended: a screen and a keyboard for the box, for this step only. The screen shows the addresses and the [certificate fingerprint](#check-the-certificate) you need. You can do everything else in the browser.

<Aside type="danger" title="This erases the box's disk">
Installing erases every file on the disk you choose. Do this only on a box that has nothing you need on it.
</Aside>

## Steps

1. Connect the network cable and, if you have them, the screen and the keyboard.
2. Put the **USB installer stick** into the box.
3. Switch the box on. It starts from the stick. If it does not, open the computer's boot menu and choose the USB stick. The computer must start in UEFI mode.
4. Wait until the box has a network address. With a cable this takes about half a minute.
5. On your laptop, open the address that the box shows. The screen of the box lists it. Without a screen, find the box in your router's list of connected devices and open its address with `http://`.
6. The page **Truchsess setup** opens. Look at the panel **Access**. Press **Refresh** if a value is missing.
7. No cable? See [Use Wi-Fi instead of a cable](#use-wi-fi-instead-of-a-cable) first.
8. In the panel **Target disk**, choose the disk. Only built-in disks are shown.
9. In the panel **Install**, tick **I understand this will erase every partition and file on the selected disk.**
10. Press **Erase and install**.
11. In the box **Erase and install?**, press **Yes, erase this disk and continue**.
12. Wait until the panel **Installation complete** appears.
13. Write down the **CEO bootstrap code** shown on the page. It looks like `ABCD-EFGH-JKLM`. You need it once, in the next step.
14. Remove the USB stick. The box restarts by itself from its own disk.

### Use Wi-Fi instead of a cable

If the box finds no cable network, it opens its own setup Wi-Fi. Its name is **Truchsess-Setup-** plus six characters. It has no password.

1. Join the setup Wi-Fi with your laptop or phone.
2. Open the setup page. Its address is shown on the box's screen (row **Setup AP URL**).
3. In the panel **WiFi**, press **Scan**.
4. Choose your office Wi-Fi under **Network**, or type its name under **Manual network name**.
5. Enter the **Password**.
6. Press **Connect WiFi**. The setup Wi-Fi turns off after about 12 seconds.
7. Join your office Wi-Fi again with your laptop and open the address the page showed you.
8. Continue with step 8 above.

## What you see afterwards

After the restart, the box is installed. Its address in your office is:

**https://truchsess.local/**

Boxes set up before October 2026 also answer on `myai.local`, so old bookmarks keep working. A box that has not yet received the update with the new name answers only on **https://myai.local/**: use that address until you update it (see [Updates](/admin/updates/#after-the-update-that-brings-the-new-address)).

Open it in your browser. You continue in [First run](/setup/first-run/).

### Check the certificate

The box makes its own security certificate. Your browser warns you once on each device, because it does not know this certificate yet.

Before you accept the warning, compare the certificate's fingerprint (a long code of letters and numbers) with the one the box shows:

- on the box's screen, after the box has started: the lines "Office address: https://truchsess.local/" and "Certificate fingerprint of this Truchsess";
- later, on the **Sign in** screen: "Certificate fingerprint of this Truchsess: ...".

The browser warns once on `truchsess.local`, and once more on `myai.local` if you also use that name.

If the two match, accept the warning. If they do not match, stop and ask support.

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Network cable | The box gets its address from your router. | Always, if you can. | Yes. |
| Wi-Fi | The box joins your office Wi-Fi. | When there is no cable. | Yes: connect again with other details. |
| Target disk | The disk Truchsess is installed on. Everything on it is erased. | The box's built-in disk. | Until you press **Yes, erase this disk and continue**. After that, no. |
| **Write install plan** | Saves the plan first. Optional: **Erase and install** does this too. | You do not need it. | Yes. |

## If something goes wrong

- "No built-in installable disk detected.": the box has no disk Truchsess can use. Ask support.
- "The WiFi network '...' requires a password.": enter the Wi-Fi password and press **Connect WiFi** again.
- "Install failed. ...": press **Copy logs** or **Download log file** in the panel **Logs** and send the file to support. The log contains your bootstrap code; do not share it with anyone else.
- The installer appears again after the restart: open the boot menu and choose **Linux Boot Manager** or the internal disk.

<NotYet>
You cannot choose another name or address for the box: it is always `truchsess.local` (and `myai.local`). You also cannot check the certificate fingerprint in the browser before the first account exists; use the box's screen for that.
</NotYet>
