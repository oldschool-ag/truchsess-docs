## Who can do this

The administrator adds the people. Each member then signs in once and sets a password.

## What you need

- The name and work e-mail of each person.
- You are in the office, on **https://truchsess.local/**. People cannot be added from the remote address.

## Steps

### Add a person

1. Open **Settings** › **People**.
2. Under **Add a person**, enter the **Name** and the **Work email**.
3. Press **Add the person**.
4. The box shows "One-time password for ... Copy it now; it is not shown again." Press **Copy** next to the password.
5. Give the one-time password to the person in a safe way (in person, or by phone), with the box's address. Do not send it in the same e-mail as the address of the box.

### The member's first sign-in

1. The member opens **https://truchsess.local/** in the office.
2. On **Sign in**, the member enters the **Work email** and the one-time **Password**.
3. The page **Change your temporary password** opens.
4. The member enters the **Temporary password**, then a **New password** (at least 12 characters, upper-case and lower-case letters and a number).
5. The member presses **Change password**.

If [remote access](/admin/remote-access/#use-the-remote-address-everyone) is on, a member can also sign in on the remote address with their e-mail and a one-time code. No password is needed there. The temporary password is still changed in the office, at the first sign-in on **https://truchsess.local/**.

### Choose who may use an agent

A new person sees the company's agents at once. To limit one agent to some people:

1. Open **Agents** and press the agent's card. Its settings open.
2. Under **For administrators**, **Who may use it**, choose **These people** and pick the people. **Everyone** also covers people you add later.

A member who wants an agent they cannot use yet presses **Add this agent** on its card under **More agents on this box**. You answer on **Home**, under **Needs you**.

## What you see afterwards

- The new person appears in **Settings** › **People** with the role "Member". Until the first sign-in the state is "Not signed in yet".
- The member sees **Home**, **Chat** and **Agents** in the menu.
- The member opens **Your account** (for example to change their password) with the picture with their initials at the top right.

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Add the person** | Creates a member account. | For each colleague. | Yes: **Remove** in the person's row (see [Users](/admin/users/#remove-a-person)). |
| **Who may use it**: **Everyone** | Every person on the box may use the agent, also people added later. | Default. | Yes. |
| **Who may use it**: **These people** | Only the people you pick may use the agent. | An agent only some people need. | Yes. |
| Product access **Member** or **Owner** | Who sees which product. A box set up with this release shows every product to everyone. Change it under **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Knowledge**, panel **Assign product access**. **Advanced** is the link **Advanced (for experts)** at the bottom of **Settings**. | Only when a product is not for everyone. | Yes: **Remove** in the panel **Product access**. |
