## Who can do this

The administrator invites the people. Each person then opens the invitation link once and sets a password.

## What you need

- The name and work e-mail of each person.
- You are in the office, on **https://truchsess.local/**. People cannot be invited from the remote address.

## Steps

### Add a person

1. Open **Settings** › **People and access**.
2. Under **Add a person**, enter the **Name** and the **Work e-mail**.
3. Keep **Member: uses the agents**. For a second administrator, choose **Administrator: also manages people, costs and the box** and type **Your own password, to invite an administrator**.
4. Tick the agents this person may use. Truchsess is always included.
5. Press **Invite** and the first name, for example **Invite Anna**.
6. Press **Send from my e-mail** or **Copy the invitation**. The box sends no e-mail: you send the invitation yourself, from your own e-mail or in a chat. The link works once, for 7 days.

More about invitations, **Send again** and **Withdraw**: [People](/admin/users/#add-a-person).

### The invitation page

1. The person opens the link from the invitation in the office, on the box's network.
2. The page says "Welcome," and their first name.
3. They type a password under **Your password** and again under **The same password again**: "Use at least 12 characters, upper- and lower-case letters, and a number."
4. They press **Set my password**.

From then on they sign in with their e-mail address and that password. If [remote access](/admin/remote-access/#use-the-remote-address-everyone) is on, they can also sign in on the remote address with their e-mail and a one-time code.

A link that no longer works says why, always with what to do:

- "This invitation has expired. Ask your administrator for a new invitation."
- "This invitation was already used to set a password. Sign in with your e-mail address and that password, or ask your administrator for a new invitation."
- "This invitation was withdrawn. Ask your administrator for a new invitation."
- "This invitation link is not valid. Ask your administrator for a new invitation."

For a new invitation, the administrator presses **Send again** on the person's row.

### Choose who may use an agent

A new person gets Truchsess and the agents you ticked. To change who may use one agent later:

1. Open **Agents** and press the agent's card. Its settings open.
2. Under **For administrators**, **Who may use it**, choose **These people** and pick the people. **Everyone** also covers people you add later.

A member who wants an agent they cannot use yet presses **Add this agent** on its card under **More agents on this box**. You answer on **Home**, under **Needs you**.

## What you see afterwards

- The new person appears in **Settings** › **People and access** with the role **Member** and, until they use the link, the state "Invited (the link works until ...)".
- The member sees **Home**, **Chat** and **Agents** in the menu.
- The member opens **Your account** (for example to change their password) with the picture with their initials at the top right.

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Invite** | Adds the person and makes an invitation link for 7 days. | For each colleague. | Yes: **Withdraw**, or **Remove** in the person's row (see [People](/admin/users/#remove-a-person)). |
| **Who may use it**: **Everyone** | Every person on the box may use the agent, also people added later. | Default. | Yes. |
| **Who may use it**: **These people** | Only the people you pick may use the agent. | An agent only some people need. | Yes. |
| Product access **Member** or **Owner** | Who sees which product. A box set up with this release shows every product to everyone. Change it under **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Knowledge**, panel **Assign product access**. **Advanced** is the link **Advanced (for experts)** at the bottom of **Settings**. | Only when a product is not for everyone. | Yes: **Remove** in the panel **Product access**. |
