## Who can do this

The administrator creates the accounts. Each member then signs in once and sets a password.

## Before you start

- The name and work e-mail of each person.
- At least one [product](/start/glossary/#product). To add one, see [Products and knowledge](/admin/products-and-knowledge/#add-a-product).
- You are in the office, on **https://truchsess.local/**. Accounts cannot be created from the remote address.

## Steps

### Create an account

1. Open **Administration**, then **People and access**.
2. In the panel **Add a user**, enter the **Name** and the **Work email**.
3. Press **Create user**.
4. The box shows a line "Temporary password for ...". Copy the password now. It is not shown again. Copy it exactly, without the space after it. A **Copy** button is coming with a later update.
5. Give the temporary password to the person in a safe way (in person, or by phone). Do not send it in the same e-mail as the address of the box.

### Give access to a product

1. On the same page, in the panel **Assign product access**, choose the **User**.
2. Choose the **Product**.
3. Choose the **Access**: **Member** or **Owner**.
4. Press **Assign access**.

### The member's first sign-in

1. The member opens **https://truchsess.local/** in the office.
2. On **Sign in**, the member enters the **Work email** and the temporary **Password**.
3. The page **Change your temporary password** opens.
4. The member enters the **Temporary password**, then a **New password** (at least 12 characters, upper-case and lower-case letters and a number).
5. The member presses **Change password**.

If [remote access](/admin/remote-access/#use-the-remote-address-everyone) is on, a member can also sign in on the remote address with their e-mail and a one-time code. No password is needed there. The temporary password is still changed in the office, at the first sign-in on **https://truchsess.local/**.

## What you see afterwards

- The new person appears in the panel **People and access** with the role "Member". Until the first sign-in the state is "Must change password".
- The assignment appears in the panel **Product access**.
- The member sees **Chat**, **Runs** and **Agents** in the navigation, and every installed agent in **Chat**. At the end of **Agents** the member finds **Your products**, the requests for a new product or another agent, and **Your requests**.
- The member opens **Your account** (to change their password) by pressing their name at the bottom of the navigation.

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Create user** | Creates a member account. | For each colleague. | Yes: **Disable**, then **Remove** (see [Users](/admin/users/#remove-a-person)). |
| Access **Member** | The person sees the product and its agents. | Default. | Yes: **Remove** in **Product access**. |
| Access **Owner** | The same as Member in this release. | When the person is responsible for the product. | Yes. |

<NotYet>
Every member sees every installed agent in **Chat**; you cannot hide an agent from a member. Install only agents everyone may use.
</NotYet>
