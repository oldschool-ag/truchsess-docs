Truchsess is a small computer for your office that runs AI workers for your company.
You open it in a web browser on your office network; there is nothing to install on your computer.
You give a worker a task in plain words, and you see the work, its cost and its result.
A worker can only do what the administrator allowed, and it asks first before it publishes, sends, spends or deploys anything.
Your work stays on the box, and the administrator decides which AI providers may see your data.

## Where to start

**You are the administrator** (you set up the box, or you received it):

1. Read [Roles](/start/roles/) to see what only you can do.
2. Follow the setup pages in order, starting with [Unbox and connect](/setup/unbox-and-connect/).
3. Keep [Administration](/admin/users/) for later changes.

**You are a member** (your administrator gave you an account):

1. Read [Your first day as a member](/start/member/): one page, from the first sign-in to your first task.
2. Then, when you need them: [Start a task](/use/start-a-task/), [Follow a run](/use/follow-a-run/) and [Your account](/use/your-account/).
