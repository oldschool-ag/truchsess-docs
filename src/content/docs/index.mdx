Truchsess is a small computer for your office that runs AI agents for your company.
You open it in a web browser on your office network; there is nothing to install on your computer.
You give an agent a task in plain words, and you see the work, its cost and its result.
An agent can only do what the administrator allowed, and it asks first before it publishes, sends, spends or deploys anything.
Your work stays on the box, and the administrator decides which AI providers may see your data.

## Where to start

**You are the administrator** (you set up the box, or you received it):

1. Read [Roles](/start/roles/) to see what only you can do.
2. Follow the setup pages in order, starting with [Unbox and connect](/setup/unbox-and-connect/).
3. Before you install agents, read [Agent rules explained](/admin/agent-rules/): what an agent may do, and good starting settings.
4. Keep [Administration](/admin/users/) for later changes.

**You are a member** (your administrator gave you an account):

1. Read [Your first day as a member](/start/member/): one page, from the first sign-in to your first task.
2. Then [Start a task](/use/start-a-task/), [Follow a run](/use/follow-a-run/) and [Use it on your phone](/use/phone-and-voice/).
3. Your password: [Your account](/use/your-account/), opened from your name at the bottom of the navigation.

## Good to know

- The portal has a navigation on the left: **Chat**, **Runs** and **Agents** for everyone, and **Approvals** and **Administration** for the administrator. On a phone it is one bar across the top.
- Words in this manual are explained in the [Glossary](/start/glossary/).
- Some things are not possible yet. They are listed with a workaround on [Not possible yet](/not-yet/).
- Something does not work? See [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/).
- What changed in each box release: [Releases](/releases/).
