**This page is for the operator of Truchsess boxes, not for customers.** A customer box uses an AI provider key (see [AI providers](/setup/ai-providers/)) or the activation code from the card (see [First run](/setup/first-run/#activate-this-box)). The ChatGPT sign-in is not available on a customer box.

On the operator's own test boxes, the AI provider panel can show "Sign in with ChatGPT (operator only)". The operator turns it on on the box itself; it is off by default. A ChatGPT Business or Enterprise plan works without more steps. A personal **Plus** or **Pro** plan needs one confirmation first, because OpenAI may train on it unless training is switched off.

## Who can do this

The administrator of an operator box, in the office on **https://truchsess.local/**. On the remote address the box answers "Enter this in the office, on the local address."

## Before you start

- The ChatGPT sign-in is turned on for this box.
- In ChatGPT, under **Settings**, **Data controls**, "Improve the model for everyone" is switched off for the account you will sign in with. The box cannot check this; you confirm it.

## Steps

1. Open the AI provider panel: **Administration** › **Agent rules**, panel **Model lanes**, **Change AI provider**.
2. Under "Sign in with ChatGPT (operator only)", press **Sign in with ChatGPT** and finish the sign-in.
3. For a Plus or Pro plan, the panel says "This is a personal ChatGPT plan (...). OpenAI may train on it unless training is switched off in ChatGPT, Settings, Data controls. The box cannot check this."
4. Tick "I switched off 'Improve the model for everyone' for ..." with your e-mail.
5. Press **Confirm**.
6. Set the data rule: **Administration** › **Agent rules**, panel **Which AI providers may see your data**, **What providers may do with your data**: **Never trained on**. Press **Save data rule**.
7. Press **Use ChatGPT on all lanes**.

## What you see afterwards

- "Personal plan ...: training switched off, confirmed by ... on .... The box cannot check this."
- The activity record has one line for the confirmation.
- The plan counts as "never trained on" (confirmed by you), never as "never kept". So it works under **Never trained on**, and is refused under **Never kept, never trained on** and under the Switzerland or EU rules.

The confirmation ends by itself when you sign out of ChatGPT on the panel, when another e-mail or plan signs in, or when the sign-in is turned off on the box. Each ending is recorded.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Tick the box first: confirm that you switched off 'Improve the model for everyone'." | Tick the box, then **Confirm**. |
| "Sign in with ChatGPT first." | Sign in, then confirm. |
| A lane is refused with "a personal ChatGPT plan may be used for training. Switch off training in ChatGPT, Settings, Data controls, ..." | Switch training off in ChatGPT, then confirm on the panel. |
| A Free or Go plan is refused. | These plans cannot be used. |

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Confirm** | Records that training is off for this e-mail and plan. | After switching training off in ChatGPT. | Yes: sign out of ChatGPT on the panel. |

<NotYet>
The box cannot read the ChatGPT training setting itself. It relies on your confirmation, and says so on the panel.
</NotYet>
