Store function id: `visibility-in-ai-search`. Price and full description: [Visibility in AI search in the store catalog](https://faivr.ai/catalog/visibility-in-ai-search).

## What it is for

Workers that look at how your company and website show up in answers of AI search tools, and suggest changes to your website's content.

## Who can do this

- The administrator subscribes, installs and sets its permissions.
- Administrators and members give it tasks in **Chat**.

## Before you start

- The box is enrolled with the store and the function is subscribed. See [Store](/admin/store/).
- The owner policy allows the permissions the worker asks for. See [Owner policy](/admin/packages/#owner-policy).

## Permissions in plain words

Your box shows the exact list before you install: **Administration**, **Packages**, **Store**, open **Permissions** under the worker. You see **Requires** (what it asks for), **Will be granted**, **Declined** and **You choose at install**, in plain words such as "Read one repository" or "Publish content outside (asks first)".

Whatever it asks for, the box guarantees:

- it works only in its own closed workspace on the box;
- it reaches only the hosts, repositories and products it was given;
- it can never merge on GitHub, push to the default branch, or create tags or releases;
- it can never read a stored key or password;
- every protected action (publish, send, spend, use credentials, deploy) asks the administrator first.

- The website the worker looks at.

## Default autonomy levels

The same for every worker in this release:

| Kind of action | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Work in its own workspace, read what it was given | On its own |
| Write what it was given (its own branches and pull requests, knowledge, workboard cards) | On its own. Knowledge and workboard changes are listed in the run's audit trail. |
| Protected actions | Asks the administrator first, every time |

A standing approval can let it do one protected action without asking. See [Autonomy levels](/admin/autonomy-levels/).

## Example tasks

Write these in **Chat** after choosing the worker. Change the details to your company.

1. "Check how our website describes what we do, and suggest clearer text for the home page."
2. "List the questions customers might ask an AI assistant about our product, and say which ones our website answers."
3. "Suggest three new pages for our website that would help AI search tools understand our offer."

If the worker lacks a permission for a task, it says so, or the box refuses the action in plain words.
