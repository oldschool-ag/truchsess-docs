A Truchsess box has two roles. Every person has their own account.

- **Administrator.** The first account on the box. The portal calls it **CEO administrator**. There is exactly one.
- **Member.** Every account the administrator creates afterwards.

**Your account** (your initials in the menu (on a phone, at the top right)) shows your name, your e-mail address and your role ("Administrator" or "Member").

## What you see in the menu

- **Members** see **Home**, **Chat** and **Agents**.
- **The administrator** also sees **Settings**. The number next to **Home** counts what waits for the administrator's decision.
- Everyone opens **Your account** from their initials in the menu (on a phone, at the top right) (button title "Your account"). **Light or dark**, **Change your password** and **Sign out** are there.

**Advanced** is the link **Advanced (for experts)** at the bottom of **Settings**.

## Your own agents

Every person has their own agents: the row **Your agents** on **Agents**. You give tasks only to the agents in it, and the box checks this before every task. A task for another agent does not start: "... is not in your agents. Add it on the Agents page; an administrator may need to allow it first."

- **Members** add an agent with **Add this agent** on its card under **More agents on this box**. The administrator answers on **Home** with **Allow** or **Not now**, and you see the answer on the card and on **Home**. Adding an agent costs you nothing; the company pays for its agents.
- **The administrator** may use every agent and adds any agent at once. An agent the administrator installs goes into the administrator's own list.
- **Who may use it**, on the agent's card, is set by the administrator: **Everyone** (also people added later) or **These people**. Truchsess, the agent that comes with the box, is always for everyone.

See [Your agents](/admin/packages/#your-agents).

New member? Start with [Your first day as a member](/start/member/).

## Product access: owner and member

The administrator can give a person access to a product as **Member** or **Owner**.
The administrator assigns it in **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Knowledge**, panel **Assign product access**.

In this release, owner and member see and do the same things in the portal.
A product owner is not an administrator: an owner cannot install agents, approve actions or change settings.

<NotYet>
There is only one administrator. You cannot add a second administrator or make a member an administrator. Ask the administrator to do administrator tasks.
</NotYet>
