Truchsess is a small computer in your office that runs AI agents for your company. You give an agent a task in plain words, and you see the work and the result. Your administrator set it up and created your account.

There are two ways in. Use the one your administrator gave you.

## Outside the office: the remote address (no password)

1. **Open the remote address** your administrator sent you, on your computer or your phone.
2. **Enter your work e-mail.**
3. **Type the one-time code** that arrives by e-mail. You are in the portal as yourself. You need no password here, and you are not asked to change one.

   No code? Look in your spam folder, and wait a few minutes before you ask again (repeated requests are slowed down). Then ask your administrator to check that your exact e-mail address is on the list: the page says "code sent" even for an address that is not allowed.
4. Continue with [Give a task](#give-a-task).

The remote address has a normal security certificate, so there is no warning. It also works in the office.

## In the office: the office address (with a password)

1. **Open the box.** On the office network, open **https://truchsess.local/** in your browser. (A box set up before October 2026 also answers on `myai.local`.)
2. **Accept the certificate warning, once.** The box makes its own security certificate, so your browser warns that the connection is not private. This is expected on this box.
   - **Chrome:** press **Advanced**, then **Proceed to truchsess.local (unsafe)**.
   - **Safari:** press **Show Details**, then **visit this website**, then confirm.

   To be sure it is your box, the **Sign in** screen shows "Certificate fingerprint of this Truchsess". Your administrator can compare it with the box.
3. **Sign in.** Enter your **Work email** and the temporary **Password** from your administrator. Press **Sign in**.
4. **Choose your password.** On **Change your temporary password**, enter the temporary password, then a **New password**: at least 12 characters, with upper-case and lower-case letters and at least one number. Press **Change password**. You stay signed in for 12 hours, then you sign in again.

## Give a task

1. **Open Chat** in the navigation on the left (on a phone: the bar at the top). You see **Chat**, **Runs** and **Agents**.
2. **Choose an agent** under **Agent**. **Agents** lists the agents you can use, each with **Open in chat**.
3. **Describe the work** in the message box, then press **Send** (or Enter; Shift+Enter is a new line). On a phone you can also speak: see [Use it on your phone](/use/phone-and-voice/).

Each message is one run: one task for one agent. Put links into the text. **Attach** takes text files only (up to 5 files, 48 KB each), not pictures or PDFs. Never paste a password or a key: the box refuses to send it.

## Follow the run

The run's card shows its state: "Created, waiting for the worker" (the agent), "Running in the package sandbox", "Running", then "Finished" with the answer and the files. "Failed" or "Cancelled" show the reason. Your own runs are also under **Runs**. A run can wait for your administrator:

- "Waiting for the CEO administrator to decide.": the agent wants to do something outside the box, for example publish or send a message. It always asks first.
- "Waiting for the owner's check": the agent finished, but your administrator reads the result first. You see it once it is accepted.

## Good to know

- **Only the administrator** approves actions, checks results, changes settings, installs or removes agents, and manages connections and accounts. Ask your administrator for these. You can ask for a new product or another agent at the end of **Agents**, under **Ask for a product or an agent**.
- **Your password** (office address only). To change it, press your name at the bottom of the navigation, then **Change your password**; see [Your account](/use/your-account/). Forgot it? Ask your administrator: they reset it and give you a one-time password. On the remote address you never need it.
- **Sign out** is at the bottom of the navigation.
