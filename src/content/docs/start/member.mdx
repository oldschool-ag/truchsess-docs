Truchsess is a small computer in your office that runs AI agents for your company. You give an agent a task in plain words, and you see the work and the result. Your administrator set it up and invited you.

There are two ways in. Use the one your administrator gave you.

## Outside the office: the remote address (no password)

1. **Open the remote address** your administrator sent you, on your computer or your phone.
2. **Enter your work e-mail.**
3. **Type the one-time code** that arrives by e-mail. You are in the portal as yourself. You need no password here. If you also work in the office, set your password there with the link from your invitation.

   No code? Look in your spam folder, and wait a few minutes before you ask again (repeated requests are slowed down). Then ask your administrator to check that your exact e-mail address is on the list: the sign-in page says a code was sent even for an address that is not allowed.
4. **Home** opens. Continue with [Give a task](#give-a-task).

The remote address has a normal security certificate, so there is no warning. It also works in the office.

## In the office: the office address (with a password)

1. **Open the box.** On the office network, open the address your administrator gave you, for example **https://truchsess.local/** or the box's own name (`truchsess-` followed by four characters and `.local`). (A box set up before October 2026 also answers on `myai.local`.) If the name does not open, see [The box is not reachable by name](/troubleshooting/#the-box-is-not-reachable-by-name).
2. **Accept the certificate warning, once.** The box makes its own security certificate, so your browser warns that the connection is not private. This is expected on this box.
   - **Chrome:** press **Advanced**, then **Proceed to** with the box's name and **(unsafe)**, for example **Proceed to truchsess.local (unsafe)**.
   - **Safari:** press **Show Details**, then **visit this website**, then confirm.

   To be sure it is your box, the **Sign in** screen shows "Certificate fingerprint of this Truchsess". Your administrator can compare it with the box.
3. **Set your password, once.** Open the link from your invitation. The page says "Welcome," and your first name. Type a password under **Your password** and again under **The same password again**: at least 12 characters, with upper-case and lower-case letters and a number. Press **Set my password**. The link works once, for 7 days; if it no longer works, the page says why: ask your administrator for a new invitation.
4. **Sign in.** From then on, enter your **Work email** and your password on **Sign in**, and press **Sign in**. **Home** opens. You stay signed in for 12 hours, then you sign in again.

## Give a task

1. **Open Chat** in the menu on the left (on a phone: the tabs under the top bar). You see **Home**, **Chat** and **Agents**.
2. **Choose an agent** under **Agent**. **Agents** shows your agents as cards under **Your agents**: click an agent's name to open its chat.
3. **Describe the work** in the message box, then press **Send** (or Enter; Shift+Enter is a new line). On a phone you can also speak: see [Use it on your phone](/use/phone-and-voice/).

Each message is one run: one task for one agent. Put links into the text. **Attach** takes PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, text files and pictures (see [Attach files in Chat](/use/attach-files/)). Never paste a password or a key: the box refuses to send it.

## Follow the run

The run's card shows its state: "Created, waiting for the agent", "Working", "Running", then "Finished" with the answer and the files. "Failed" or "Cancelled" show the reason. Your recent tasks are also on the agent's card on **Agents** (under **Recent tasks**), and your finished tasks on **Home**, under **Recent results**. A run can wait for your administrator:

- "Waiting for the CEO administrator to decide.": the agent wants to do something outside the box, for example publish or send a message. It always asks first.
- "Waiting for the owner's check": the agent finished, but your administrator reads the result first. You see it once it is accepted.

## Good to know

- **Only the administrator** approves actions, checks results, changes settings, installs or removes agents, and manages the company's connections and accounts. Ask your administrator for these.
- **Another agent.** On **Agents**, under **More agents on this box**, press **Add this agent** on its card. Some agents are added at once: "Added. ... is in your agents." For the others your ask goes to the administrator, and the card says "Asked" with the name and the time. You see the answer on the card and on **Home**.
- **Your account** is your initials in the menu (on a phone, at the top right) (button title "Your account"). It holds **Light or dark**, **Change your password** and **Sign out**, and your own mailbox and calendars: see [Your account](/use/your-account/), [Connect your company mailbox](/use/email/) and [Connect your calendars](/use/calendars/).
- **Your password** (office address only). Change it on **Your account**, **Change your password**. Forgot it? Ask your administrator: they reset it and give you a one-time password. On the remote address you never need it.
