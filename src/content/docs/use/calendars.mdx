Connect your calendars, as many as you use. "Home shows today's and tomorrow's meetings from all of them, and an agent you give a task can see your meetings and when you are free."

Read only: "Agents only read: they never create, change, accept, decline or delete anything. Only you see your calendars." The administrator sees only how many people connected how many calendar accounts, never a name, a server or an entry.

## What you need

- **Apple iCloud:** your Apple Account e-mail and an app-specific password (the steps are below).
- **Hostpoint or another hoster (CalDAV):** the calendar server, your user name and your password. For Hostpoint the server is **caldav.hostpoint.ch** and the user name is your e-mail address. Another hoster names its server in its help pages.
- You are in the office, on **https://truchsess.local/**.

## Steps

### Apple iCloud: create an app-specific password

1. Sign in at **account.apple.com**.
2. Open **Sign-In and Security**, then **App-Specific Passwords**.
3. Choose **Generate an app-specific password**, name it Truchsess, and copy the password Apple shows (it looks like abcd-efgh-ijkl-mnop).

Your normal Apple password never goes into the box.

### Connect a calendar

1. Press the picture with your initials at the top right. The page **Your account** opens.
2. In **Your connections**, panel **Calendars**, press **Add calendar**.
3. Press **Apple iCloud** or **Another calendar (for example Hostpoint)**.
4. iCloud: type your **Apple Account e-mail** and the **App-specific password**. Another calendar: type the **Calendar server**, the **User name (for Hostpoint: your e-mail address)** and the **Password**.
5. Press **Test and connect**.

"Truchsess tests the connection before saving it. It only reads your calendars."

### Choose which calendars agents see

1. On the connection's card, under **Show on Home and to your agents**, tick the calendars to show.
2. Press **Save**.

Home and your agents read only the ticked calendars.

## What you see afterwards

A card for each connection: "Connected", with the time it was last read, the list of its calendars with tick boxes, and a line that names the agents that read them. Home shows today's and tomorrow's meetings.

To remove a connection: **Disconnect**, then confirm: "Disconnect ... ? Home and your agents no longer see these calendars, and the box deletes its access at once."

You can connect up to 10 calendar accounts.

## If it does not work

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Fill in every field." | Type the server (for Another calendar), the user name and the password. |
| "The calendar could not be connected." | Check the server, the user name and the password. For iCloud use the app-specific password, not your Apple password. |
| "The connection stopped working (for example after a password change). Connect again so Home and your agents see these calendars." | Press **Connect again** and type the new password. |
| "Google is not set up on this box yet. Your administrator sets it up once." or the same for Microsoft 365 | The button is greyed out. See the box below. |
| "For safety, passwords, keys and other secrets are entered only in the office." | Connect the calendar in the office. |

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Apple iCloud** | Connects your iCloud calendars with an app-specific password. | You use an iPhone or Mac calendar. | Yes: **Disconnect**. |
| **Another calendar (for example Hostpoint)** | Connects a CalDAV calendar of your hoster. | Your company mail is at Hostpoint or another hoster. | Yes: **Disconnect**. |
| Tick boxes, **Save** | Chooses which calendars Home and your agents see. | You keep a private calendar in the same account. | Yes: change the ticks. |
