Everyone has the page **Your account**: members and administrators. Open it with the picture with your initials (button title "Your account"), next to your name. On a phone it is in the bar across the top.

The top of the page shows your name, your e-mail address and your role.

The page holds:

- **Light or dark**: how the portal looks for you.
- **Change your password**.
- **Sign out**.
- **Your connections**: "Your own mailbox and calendars. Only your agents use them, and only for your tasks." With the panels **Email inbox** and **Calendars**: see [Connect your company mailbox](/use/email/), [Add a private mailbox](/use/private-mailbox/) and [Connect your calendars](/use/calendars/).
- **What Truchsess has learned**: what each agent learned from you. "Only you see your lessons; administrators do not." See [What Truchsess has learned](/use/what-truchsess-learned/).

## Who can do this

Everyone, for their own account. Changing the password, and connecting a mailbox or a calendar, work only in the office, on **https://truchsess.local/**.

## What you need

- For a new password: your current password, and you are in the office.

## Steps

### Change your password

1. Press the picture with your initials. The page **Your account** opens.
2. Under **Change your password**, enter the **Current password**.
3. Enter the **New password**: at least 12 characters, with upper-case and lower-case letters and a number. It must differ from the current one.
4. Enter it again under **New password again**.
5. Press **Change password**.

### Light or dark

Under **Light or dark**, press **Light** or **Dark**. The choice is kept in this browser.

### Sign out

Press **Sign out** on **Your account**.

## What you see afterwards

"Password changed. Your other signed-in browsers are signed out." This browser stays signed in.

## If it does not work

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "The current password is incorrect." | Enter your current password again. |
| "The two new passwords are not the same." | Type the new password twice in the same way. |
| "Choose a new password that differs from the current one." | Choose another password. |
| "Use a password of at least 12 characters." | Choose a longer password. |
| "On the remote address you sign in with your e-mail code; your password is changed in the office, on the local address." | You are on the remote address. Change the password in the office. |
| You forgot your password. | Ask your administrator. They give you a one-time password; see [Users](/admin/users/). |

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Change password** | Sets a new password and signs out your other browsers. | Whenever you want, or when you think someone knows it. | Change it again. |
| **Light** or **Dark** | Changes how the portal looks. | As you like. | Yes. |
| **Sign out** | Signs you out in this browser. | When you leave a shared computer. | Sign in again. |
