Your agents can read your company mail and write replies as drafts in your **Drafts** folder. They never send: you read each draft and send it yourself.

You connect your own mailbox on **Your account** › **Email inbox**. Only you and the tasks you give can use it. The administrator never reads your mail.

For a mailbox only you should see, for example your private Gmail, see [Add a private mailbox](/use/private-mailbox/).

## What you need

- Your company e-mail address. It must be in one of the company's mail domains, or on the administrator's list of company mailboxes. The administrator sets these: see [Company mail](/admin/company-mail/).
- The password of that mailbox. Gmail, Google Workspace and some other providers need an app password instead: the form says so and shows the steps. See [When the form asks for an app password](#when-the-form-asks-for-an-app-password).
- You are in the office, on **https://truchsess.local/**. On the remote address the box refuses: "For safety, passwords, keys and other secrets are entered only in the office."

## Steps

### Connect your first company mailbox

1. Press the picture with your initials at the top right. The page **Your account** opens.
2. Scroll to **Your connections**, panel **Email inbox**. The form **Connect your company mailbox** is open.
3. Type your address under **Email address**. If it is your sign-in address, it is already filled in.
4. Check the **Mail server**. It shows "Set by your administrator". You cannot change it.
5. Type the mailbox password under **Password**. It is "Stored encrypted on the box." For a company domain on Gmail or Google Workspace, the field is **App password (16 characters from Google)** instead: see [below](#gmail-or-google-workspace-the-app-password).
6. Press **Test and connect**.

"Truchsess tests the connection before saving it." The button reads "Testing the connection" while it works.

### Add another company mailbox

You can connect several company mailboxes, one per address, for example your own address and a shared one like the office address.

1. On **Your account** › **Email inbox**, press **Add another company mailbox**. It is below the list of your mailboxes, next to **Add a private mailbox (only you)**. (With no company mailbox yet, it reads **Add a company mailbox**.)
2. Type the address under **Email address**.
3. Type its password, or the app password the form asks for.
4. Press **Test and connect**.

Only addresses in the company's mail domains can be connected, and never another person's address.

### When the form asks for an app password

Some mail providers do not let other programs use your normal password. For them the form names the password it needs, shows the provider's steps and a link, and says "Type it only here, never into a chat." This is the same on every mailbox form: company and private, for members and administrators.

| Provider | The field | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gmail and Google Workspace | **App password (16 characters from Google)** | See [Gmail or Google Workspace](#gmail-or-google-workspace-the-app-password) below. |
| iCloud Mail | **App-specific password (from Apple)** | At account.apple.com, choose Sign-In and Security, then App-Specific Passwords, and create one. |
| Yahoo Mail | **App password (from Yahoo)** | At login.yahoo.com/account/security, choose Generate app password, and create one. |
| AOL Mail | **App password (from AOL)** | At login.aol.com/account/security, choose Generate app password, and create one. |
| GMX | **Password or app password (from GMX)** | In GMX, open the settings, POP3/IMAP access, and allow access over IMAP. With two-factor sign-in, create an application-specific password there; without it, use your GMX password. |
| WEB.DE | **Password or app password (from WEB.DE)** | The same as GMX, in WEB.DE's settings. |

Microsoft 365 and Outlook do not use an app password: they get **Connect with Microsoft**, which is coming soon (see below).

### Gmail or Google Workspace: the app password

Gmail and Google Workspace need an app password, not your Google password. This holds for a company mailbox on Google Workspace and for a private Gmail.

1. In your Google account, turn on 2-Step Verification, if it is not on yet.
2. Open **myaccount.google.com/apppasswords** (the link is on the form) and sign in.
3. Type a name, for example Truchsess, and press **Create**.
4. Copy the 16 characters Google shows.
5. On **Your account** › **Email inbox**, type your address, and paste the 16 characters into **App password (16 characters from Google)**.
6. Press **Test and connect**.

Type the app password only into the box, never into a chat, a task or an e-mail.

### Disconnect a mailbox

1. On the mailbox's card, press **Disconnect**.
2. Confirm. The page asks: "Disconnect ... ? Agents can no longer use it, and its password is deleted from the box at once."

Each mailbox has its own **Disconnect**. The other mailboxes stay connected.

## What you see afterwards

- The line "... is connected."
- A card for the mailbox with **Type** (for example "Email inbox · IMAP" and the server), **Status** "Connected", **Connected** (the date), and **Agents may**: "Read your mail and write drafts. They never send."
- A line that names the agents that read it, for example "Penn reads this mailbox for your tasks." Or "None of your agents reads this mailbox yet."
- **Open your drafts**, when your administrator entered the address of the company's Drafts folder on the web.

With two or more company mailboxes, an agent works on them as one inbox: "An agent replies from the mailbox a mail came to, with the signature for its domain. For a new mail it asks you which mailbox to use. What it learns about how you write comes from your company mail only." On Home, each mail shows the address it came to.

## What agents may do

"Read your mail and write drafts. Agents never send, delete, move or mark mail; reading leaves it unread."

An agent reads your mailbox only for a task you give it. Before an agent reads your mail for the first time, it asks you: see [When an agent needs something first](/use/agent-needs-something/).

### Your company signature

When the administrator collects signature samples for the company standard, your card shows "Company signature: not shared. Nobody's task reads your sent mail for it." To share, press **Yes, share the closing lines of my last 5 sent mails**. An administrator's task then reads only those closing lines, never the mails themselves. **Stop sharing my signature** takes it back.

## If it does not work

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "The administrator has not set the company's mail domains yet, so no company mailbox can be connected." | Ask your administrator to set the company mail domains: [Company mail](/admin/company-mail/). |
| "... is not a company address (...). For your own mailbox, use Add a private mailbox (only you)." | The address is not in a company domain. For a private address, use [Add a private mailbox](/use/private-mailbox/). |
| "... is already connected." | The mailbox is on your page already. |
| "The mailbox could not be connected." | Check the password and press **Test and connect** again. For Gmail or Google Workspace: use an app password, not your Google password. |
| "Enter the mailbox password." or "Enter the app password." | Type the password or the app password, then press **Test and connect**. |
| The test fails for a Google Workspace mailbox, although you pasted a new app password. | Your Google Workspace administrator may have switched app passwords off. They switch them on in the Google Admin console. |
| Card: "The connection stopped working (for example after a password change). Connect again to let agents use your mailbox." | You changed the mailbox password. Disconnect the mailbox and connect it again with the new password. |
| Card: "The company changed its mail server. Connect it again so agents can use it." | Press **Connect again** and type the password. **Remove** takes it off the list. |
| "For safety, passwords, keys and other secrets are entered only in the office." | Connect the mailbox in the office, on **https://truchsess.local/**. |

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Test and connect** | Tests the password and connects the mailbox. | Once per mailbox. | Yes: **Disconnect**. |
| **Add another company mailbox** | Connects one more company address. | You also read a shared or second address. | Yes: **Disconnect** on its card. |
| **Disconnect** | Agents stop using the mailbox; the password is deleted at once. | You no longer want agents in that mailbox. | Connect it again. |
| **Yes, share the closing lines of my last 5 sent mails** | Lets an administrator's task read your signature lines. | The company builds one signature standard. | Yes: **Stop sharing my signature**. |
