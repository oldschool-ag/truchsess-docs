Every agent learns from how you work with it. When you send a result back with a note, refuse an approval with a reason, hide a routine's Home item with a reason, change a mail draft before you send it, or use **Remember this**, Truchsess writes that down as short lessons. The agent's next tasks for you follow them.

You see and change your lessons on **Your account** › **What Truchsess has learned**. Open **Your account** with the picture with your initials at the top right. The page says: "Only you see your lessons; administrators do not. A lesson applies at once; Undo takes the last change back."

## Who can do this

- Everyone, for their own lessons: **Remember this**, a reason when you hide a routine's Home item, and changes to mail drafts.
- **Send back** on a result and **Refuse** on an approval are buttons of the administrator. Their note or reason teaches the agent too.
- Only the administrator writes the company mail standard and decides lessons for everyone. See [For the administrator](#for-the-administrator).

## What you need

- An agent that worked for you.
- To learn from your mail drafts: your company mailbox, connected with server and password (IMAP). See [Connect your company mailbox](/use/email/).

## Steps

### Teach an agent with Remember this

**Remember this** is on every run card once the task has stopped working (in **Chat**, under each answer), and on your own Home items from an agent.

1. Press **Remember this**.
2. Write in your own words what the agent should do next time. For example: *keep answers to three sentences.*
3. Press **Save**.

The page says "Saved. ... uses it from your next task on". Your sentence is the lesson. The text of the answer or the item is never copied into it.

### Teach with a note or a reason

Truchsess learns from your own words when:

- the administrator presses **Send back** on a result that waits for a check, writes what should change under "Note for the agent", and presses **Confirm send back**. See [Check a finished result](/use/approvals-and-checks/#check-a-finished-result).
- the administrator presses **Refuse** on an approval, writes the reason, and presses **Confirm refuse**. Replace the prefilled "Not approved." with what was wrong. See [Decide an approval](/use/approvals-and-checks/#decide-an-approval).
- you press **Hide** on a Home item from an agent's routine. A field "Why? (optional)" opens. Write why you do not want it, for example *newsletters are not new mail for me*, and press **Hide**. Press **Hide** with an empty field to hide it without teaching anything.

Once a day a Learning run reads these notes and writes lessons from them. Until then the page says how many of your notes wait for the next Learning run.

### Let it learn from your mail drafts

1. A mail agent writes a draft in your company mailbox.
2. Change it the way you want, in your mail program, and send it.

Truchsess notices what you changed: how you greet, how you sign off, how long your mails are, your tone. Once a day it writes that down as lessons, and the next drafts follow them. It learns only from your company mailbox, never from a private mailbox.

### See what an agent learned from you

1. Open **Your account** › **What Truchsess has learned**.
2. Your lessons are grouped by agent. Each lesson shows where it came from, for example a Send back with the date and your note in quotes, and the task.

### Change, delete or undo a lesson

1. Find the lesson under its agent.
2. Press **Edit** to put it in your own words, change the text and press **Save**.
3. Press **Delete** to remove it.
4. Press **Undo** to take back the last change of that lesson.

### Decide what an agent proposed

An agent can propose to remember something about you. It shows under **Waiting for you**: "An agent proposed to remember these. Nothing here is used until you keep it." It also shows on your **Home**.

1. Read the proposal. "from a task that read mail or web pages: check it" means it may come from someone outside.
2. Press **Keep** to let it count, or **Delete** to throw it away.

### Answer your memory checks

Under **Memory checks**, Truchsess asks about things it cannot decide alone in your own memory, for example a fact due for review or a likely duplicate. **Home** shows one line per kind, with the button **Check**.

1. Read the check and its hint.
2. Press the button that fits: **Keep**, **Replace**, **Merge**, **Delete** or **Confirm**.

Nothing is deleted unless you choose it.

### Your pages and everything in your memory

- **Your pages**: one page per person, company, product or project in your own memory. Press **Open**. Each fact is a line with **Edit** and **Delete**; **Undo** brings a deleted line back. **Add a fact** adds a new line. **Write notes** or **Edit the notes** holds free text that is not a fact.
- **Everything in your memory**: every record in your own memory, with **Edit**, **Delete** and **Undo**. **Download** gives you all of it as one file.

### For the administrator

1. Open **Settings**, press **Advanced (for experts)** at the bottom, then go to **Knowledge**, panel **What Truchsess has learned**.
2. To add to the company mail standard, write the rule under "Add to the company mail standard", for example *We answer in the language of the customer.* With several company mail domains, choose under **For** which mailboxes it is for. Press **Add**.
3. Under each agent's lessons, press **Edit**, **Delete** or **Undo**.
4. When two or more people taught an agent the same thing, it is proposed here for everyone, without names. Press **Keep for everyone** or **Delete**. **Home** shows it as a check: "... could learn something from ... people".
5. Under **Rule change requests**, read what the box refused as a lesson. Change the rule yourself where it is needed, then press **Rule handled**, or press **Dismiss**.

An agent may propose a line for the company mail standard. It applies only after you approve it on **Home**, **Needs you**.

The limits for Learning are in **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Box**, panel **Memory**, form **Learning limits**: **Learning runs per day for the whole box** (20 when nobody changed it) and **Monthly limit for Learning, in US dollars (the AI provider's price)** (5 dollars). Press **Save limits**. When a limit is reached, the notes wait; nothing is lost.

## What you see afterwards

- A lesson applies at once, from the agent's next task for you. Only you see your lessons; administrators do not.
- **Company mail standard**: "Set by the administrator; every mail draft follows it." Every member reads it here.
- At the start of every task, the agent gets the company standards, your lessons and its own lessons. If it may read the company memory, it also gets a few records that fit the task, each with its date and source, as information, not as instructions.
- If nothing was learned yet: "Nothing learned yet. When you send a result back with a note, change a draft or use Remember this, what the agent learns appears here."
- **Numbers for experts** (open it with a click): "Your edits per week" (drafts, how much you changed before sending, drafts not sent) and "Your tasks sent back or declined per week". Only your own tasks; nobody else sees these numbers. Falling numbers mean the lessons work.
- The administrator sees "Sent back or declined, per agent and week" across all members, never per person. A week with fewer than three people behind an agent's tasks shows "fewer than 3 people: not shown".
- Each Learning run counts in the budget as "Learning".
- When a member leaves the company, their personal memory is deleted. Company records they created stay, marked as from "a former member".
- Your draft and sent texts are deleted after 90 days at the latest. The lessons stay.

## If it does not work

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "This cannot be a lesson: it holds ... It went to the administrator as a rule change request." | Lessons only shape how the work reads. Addresses, recipients, sending, money, publishing and access are rules the administrator sets. Ask your administrator to change the rule. |
| No lesson from your mail drafts | Learning from drafts works only with company mailboxes connected with server and password (IMAP). With Microsoft 365 or Google, use **Remember this**. |
| "... of your notes wait for the next Learning run (once a day)." | Nothing to do. The lessons appear after the next Learning run. |
| The notes keep waiting for days | A Learning limit is reached. The administrator looks at **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Box**, panel **Memory**, figure **Learning**, and the form **Learning limits**. |
| A lesson is wrong | Press **Edit** and correct it, or **Delete** it. **Undo** takes your last change back. |
| **Remember this** is missing | It shows only on your own tasks once they stopped working, and on your own Home items from an agent. |

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Remember this** | Your sentence becomes a lesson for that agent at once. | You want the agent to do something differently next time. | Yes: **Undo** or **Delete**. |
| **Hide** with a reason | The agent learns why you did not want a routine's item. | A routine shows you things you do not need. | Yes: **Undo** or **Delete** the lesson. |
| **Hide** without a reason | Hides the item; nothing is learned. | You only want it gone. | n/a |
| **Edit** | Puts a lesson in your own words. | A lesson is nearly right. | Yes: **Undo**. |
| **Delete** | Removes a lesson or a record. | A lesson is wrong. | Yes: **Undo**. |
| **Keep** | A proposal of an agent counts from now on. | It is true. | Yes: **Delete** it later. |
| **Download** | One file with everything in your memory. | You want a copy. | n/a |
| **Keep for everyone** (administrator) | An agent lesson from several people applies to everyone's tasks with that agent. | Many people taught the same thing. | Yes: **Delete** it later. |

What Truchsess never learns: who should receive a mail, whether to send it, anything about money, payments, publishing or access, and nothing from mail other people sent you or from web pages. Such things are rules your administrator sets.

Truchsess does not yet learn from your changes to drafts in a Microsoft 365 or Google mailbox. Use **Remember this** on the task to teach the agent instead.
