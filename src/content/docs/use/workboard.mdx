The [workboard](/start/glossary/#workboard) keeps work cards for each product. A card has a title, a description and a status: to do, in progress, blocked or done.

## Who can do this

Administrators and members, through a worker that has workboard permission.

## Before you start

- The worker has "Read the workboard" to list and read cards, and "Change the workboard" to create and update cards. You see this under "N permissions · show" in **Chat**.
- The worker works on the product whose board you mean.

## Steps

1. Open **Chat** and choose a worker with workboard permission.
2. Ask in plain words, for example:
   - "List the open cards on the board."
   - "Create a card: Update the price page. Status: to do."
   - "Set the card about the login bug to done."
3. Press **Send**.

## What you see afterwards

- The worker answers with the cards, or confirms what it changed and gives the card's id.
- The run's **Audit** lists every card the worker read, created or changed. The card text is not copied into the audit.
- A worker sees and changes only cards on the boards of its own products.

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| List or read cards | Shows up to 100 cards of a product. | To see the state of work. | n/a |
| Create a card | Adds a card with the status "to do" unless you name another. | New work. | Ask a worker to set it to done. |
| Change a card | Changes the title, the description or the status. | Progress. | Ask a worker to change it back. |

<NotYet>
The portal has no workboard page: you cannot see, sort or edit cards yourself, and a card cannot be deleted. Ask a worker with workboard permission to list or change cards for you.
</NotYet>
