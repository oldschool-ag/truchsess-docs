---
title: When an agent needs something first
description: Before an agent runs, it says what it still needs, for example your mailbox, a repository or a website, with one button. Nothing runs and nothing is charged until then.
---

Some agents need something before they can work for you: Penn needs your mailbox, a developer agent (Bob) needs a repository, a website agent (Gideon) needs a website. The agent says so before it runs, not after.

You see one plain line and one button wherever the agent is: in **Chat** before you type, on its card on **Agents**, on **Home** under **Needs you**, and on a task that stopped.

## Who can do this

- **Your mailbox:** only you. Nobody else can say yes for your mailbox, and you cannot say yes for a colleague's.
- **A repository or a website:** an administrator chooses it.

## What you need

- For your mailbox: your company mailbox, or the details to connect it. See [Connect your company mailbox](/use/email/).

## Steps

### Let Penn read your mailbox

1. Open Penn in **Chat**. Before you type anything, Penn says what it still needs, for example "Penn cannot read your mailbox yet."
2. Press **Let Penn read your mailbox**.

One click does everything that is missing:

- it records your own yes;
- if you are an administrator, it allows mailbox reading for Penn on this box;
- if your mailbox is not connected yet, it opens **Your account** › **Email inbox**: "Connect your mailbox. Penn starts your request as soon as it is connected." Connect it as usual.

Afterwards the line reads "Penn may read your mailbox now."

### If you already typed a request

Your request is kept: "Your request waits: ... It runs by itself as soon as Penn can." When you have given what is missing, it runs by itself. A kept request waits up to 24 hours.

### A repository or a website

The line says, for example, "Bob has no repository to work on yet." or "Gideon has no website to look at yet."

- **Administrator:** press **Choose Bob's repository** (or **Choose Gideon's website**). The agent's card opens; choose it there.
- **Member:** the line ends with the administrator who chooses it. Ask that administrator.

## What you see afterwards

The line goes away and the agent works. A request you typed before runs by itself.

Nothing runs and nothing is charged while something is missing. No answer and no file is made.

Penn reads mail for you only after you have connected your own mailbox. That holds for every task you give it, even one that does not need your mail.

## If it does not work

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Penn may not read mailboxes on this box yet. ... decides." with a button **Ask ...** | Your company has not allowed mailbox reading yet. Press the button: it asks your administrator. |
| "Only the person whose mailbox it is can say yes." | Each person says yes for their own mailbox. Ask the colleague to open Penn themselves. |
| Your kept request did not run | It waits up to 24 hours. Type it again. |

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Let Penn read your mailbox** | Your yes, the company's allow (administrator) and the mailbox connect, in one. | You want Penn to work with your mail. | Yes: **Disconnect** the mailbox on [Your account](/use/your-account/). |
| **Ask** your administrator | Sends your ask to the administrator's Home. | Mailbox reading is not allowed on the box yet. | Not needed. |
