[Knowledge](/start/glossary/#knowledge) is the set of documents of a product that workers may read, for example a handbook or product notes. The administrator imports them. See [Products and knowledge](/admin/products-and-knowledge/#import-documents).

## Who can do this

Administrators and members, through a worker that has permission to read the product's knowledge.

## Before you start

- The administrator has imported documents for the product.
- The worker has the permission "Read one product's knowledge" for that product. You see it under the link "N permissions · show" in **Chat**.

## Steps

1. Open **Chat** and choose a worker with knowledge permission.
2. Ask in plain words, for example "Search the product knowledge for our refund rules and quote them."
3. Press **Send**.

## What you see afterwards

- The worker searches and reads the documents and answers in its reply.
- The run's **Audit** lists every document the worker read or wrote, with the document's key. It does not copy the text into the audit.
- A worker that may also write knowledge ("Write one product's knowledge") can save a new version of a document. The earlier version stays readable.

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ask a worker to search or read | The worker reads documents of its products only. | Questions about the product. | n/a |
| Ask a worker to write | Saves a new version of a document. | Only with a worker that may write. | Partly: the earlier version stays, and a worker can write it back as the newest version. |
| Ask for an earlier version | A worker can read an older version of a document. | To compare or restore text. | n/a |

A worker that reads or writes knowledge always uses an AI provider that never trains on your data.

<NotYet>
You cannot browse, search or read the knowledge yourself in the portal, see the list of older versions, or delete a document. Ask a worker to search, list or quote documents for you. To replace a document, the administrator imports a new version.
</NotYet>
