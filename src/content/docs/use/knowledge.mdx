[Knowledge](/start/glossary/#knowledge) is the set of documents of a product that agents may read, for example a handbook or product notes. The administrator imports them under **Settings** › **Your company**, part **Products and documents**: **Open** on the product, then the panel **Import documents**. See [Products and knowledge](/admin/products-and-knowledge/#import-documents).

Agents find two kinds of things:

- **Product documents**, on this page. An agent finds them by the words in them, so ask with the words the documents use.
- **The company memory**: facts, one knowledge page per person, company, product or project, and what people taught the agents. An agent finds these by meaning, not only by the words you type. Ask *what does our small plan cost*, and the agent finds the page with the plan's name and its price, even though that page never says *small*. This works in German and English, and it runs entirely on your box: nothing is sent anywhere to be indexed.

## Who can do this

Administrators and members, through an agent that has permission to read the product's knowledge or to search the company memory.

## What you need

- The administrator has imported documents for the product.
- The agent has the permission "Read one product's knowledge" for that product. You see it under the link "... permissions · show" in **Chat**, or on the agent's card on **Agents**.
- To search the company memory, the agent has the permission "Search the company memory".
- For search by meaning: it is switched on by itself. A new box first downloads its search model and reads through the memory; until then, the memory is found by the exact words. The administrator sees the state under **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Box**. See [Search by meaning](/admin/products-and-knowledge/#search-by-meaning).

## Steps

1. Open **Chat** and, under **Agent**, choose an agent with knowledge permission.
2. Ask in plain words, for example: *Search the product knowledge for our refund rules and quote them.* Or, for the company memory: *What do we know about this customer?*
3. Press **Send**.

## What you see afterwards

- The agent searches and reads the documents and answers in its reply.
- The run's audit trail lists every document the agent read or wrote, with the document's key. It does not copy the text into the audit. The administrator sees it with **Audit** in **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Activity and audit trail**, panel **Run history**.
- An agent that may also write knowledge ("Write one product's knowledge") can save a new version of a document. The earlier version stays readable.
- By default a knowledge write is at the level "Tells you after": the administrator gets a notice on **Home**, in **Recent results**. If the administrator raised it to "Asks you first", nothing is written until the administrator approves. See [Approvals and checks](/use/approvals-and-checks/#decide-a-held-write).

## How Truchsess learns

Since release 2026.10.25-af84ac2 the company memory learns by rules:

- A fact an agent learns in a clean task goes into company memory at once, marked "not checked yet". It ranks below anything a person entered.
- These wait for a person, on an approval card that says why: a fact from a mail, a web page, pasted text or an attached file; a fact that contradicts what a person entered; and a judgment about a person.
- On Monday, administrators see "This week the company learned" so many facts on **Home**, with **Undo** for each.

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ask an agent to search or read | The agent reads documents of its products only. | Questions about the product. | n/a |
| Ask an agent to write | Saves a new version of a document. | Only with an agent that may write. | Partly: the earlier version stays, and an agent can write it back as the newest version. |
| Ask for an earlier version | An agent can read an older version of a document. | To compare or restore text. | n/a |
| Ask an agent to search the company memory | The agent finds facts and pages by meaning, in German or English. | Questions about customers, contacts, prices or how the company works. | n/a |

An agent that reads or writes knowledge always uses an AI provider that never trains on your data.
