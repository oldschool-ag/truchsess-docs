Truchsess is the agent included in your box. You do not buy or install it. It answers everyone in your company about the box: how something works, where a setting is, what happened in a run, and which agent could do a job for your company. You meet it in step 4 of the first setup. See [Set up your box](/setup/first-run/#meet-truchsess).

Truchsess answers from four sources only: the manual of the release your box runs, the map of every page and button of the portal, facts about your box, and a run you ask about. It reads them with your rights. It answers in the language of your question, and names places and buttons exactly as the portal shows them.

## Who can do this

Everyone with an account on the box. A member gets answers about their own runs, connections and agents. Questions about the company's settings, keys and codes, the budget and the costs, the audit or other people's runs are only for the administrator: Truchsess then says so and gives a short text to send to the administrator. Only an administrator sees the starter credit.

## What you need

- An account on the box.
- For answers written by the AI: the box's AI is set up, with the managed AI from the activation or an OpenRouter key of your own. Without it, Truchsess answers from the manual only.

## Steps

### Ask Truchsess in Chat

1. Open **Chat**. Or, on **Home** or **Agents**, press **Ask Truchsess** on the card **Truchsess**.
2. Choose **Truchsess (included)** in the list of agents. It is the first entry. On a box without other agents it is already chosen.
3. Type your question under **Question for Truchsess**, for example which agent could answer your customer mails.
4. Press **Send**.
5. Wait for the answer: "Truchsess is looking this up…".

### Ask from any page

1. Press the question mark **Ask Truchsess** in the menu. The panel **Truchsess · Help** opens: "For questions about this box. Work for agents goes in Chat."
2. Type your question and press **Ask**.

The panel and **Chat** show the same conversation. In the panel, Truchsess also knows which page you are on.

## What you see afterwards

- **Home**: under **Your agents**, the first card is **Truchsess**, "Included in your box", "Answers everyone's questions about the box and helps choose the right agent." An administrator also sees "Starter credit left: CHF ...". The button **Ask Truchsess** opens Chat with Truchsess.
- **Agents**: the same card, first under **Your agents**.
- **Chat**: "Truchsess · included in your box" above the conversation, and the note "Truchsess answers from the manual, the settings and this box. It uses the box's AI credit."
- Your conversation is your own. The box keeps its last 40 messages. **New conversation** in Chat, or **Clear the conversation** in the panel, deletes it.

### The starter credit

The activation with the code from the card brings a one-time starter credit for the AI. The amount is the one your code carries; step 2 of the first setup names it, and the card **Truchsess** shows what is left. Truchsess and your agents use the same credit. There is no monthly reset.

Each answer of Truchsess counts in the costs as the row "Truchsess". When the starter credit is used up, new runs pause and Truchsess answers from the manual only. The administrator then requests a top-up or switches to an own AI key. See [Budget](/admin/budget/#the-starter-credit).

Truchsess answers with the AI of the Standard lane, and only through providers that meet your company's data rule.

## If it does not work

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "The AI of this box is not set up yet, so Truchsess answers from the manual only." | The administrator activates the box or adds an AI key under **Settings** › **Advanced** › **AI**. **Advanced** is the link **Advanced (for experts)** at the bottom of **Settings**. |
| "Truchsess answers with an OpenRouter key, and this box has none, so it answers from the manual only." | The box uses another AI provider. Truchsess writes answers with the AI only through the managed AI or an OpenRouter key. |
| "The administrator has switched Truchsess off on this box, so here is what the manual and the settings map say." | Ask the administrator, or read the places and manual pages the answer lists. |
| "No provider of the Standard lane meets the company's data rule (Settings > Advanced > Agent rules), so Truchsess answers from the manual only." | The administrator checks the data rule and the Standard lane. See [AI providers and lanes](/admin/ai-providers-and-lanes/). |
| "The AI did not answer just now, so here is what the manual and the settings map say. Try again in a moment." | Ask again in a moment. |
| "This looks like ... Truchsess never needs it, so it was not sent and not kept." | You typed something that looks like a key, a password or a code. Truchsess never needs one. Type it only in the field the message names. |
| "I can't answer that for you: ... only for the administrator." | Send the text the answer gives you to your administrator. |
| "I could not find an exact answer in this box's sources." | Look at the three places the answer names, or write to support at the address it gives. |

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | Who | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Ask Truchsess answers with AI** (**Settings** › **Advanced** › **Box**, panel **Ask Truchsess**) | On: everyone can ask Truchsess. Off: "Ask Truchsess searches the manual and the settings only, without AI, and costs nothing." | Administrator | Yes, tick it again. |
| **New conversation** or **Clear the conversation** | Deletes your conversation with Truchsess. | Everyone, for their own | No. |

<NotYet>
Truchsess changes nothing on the box. It says where to go and what to press, and you do it yourself. It does not install agents: an administrator does that under **Agents**, **Store** (see [Store](/admin/store/)). It does no work for your company, such as reading mail or writing posts: give such work to an installed agent in **Chat** (see [Start a task](/use/start-a-task/)). You cannot attach files to a question for Truchsess.
</NotYet>
