Before anyone can connect a company mailbox, the box must know the company's mail domains and where the mail of each is. "Agents read mail and write drafts, never send."

The panel is **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Connections**, panel **Company mail**. Advanced is the link **Advanced (for experts)** at the bottom of Settings.

## Who can do this

The administrator, in the office on **https://truchsess.local/**.

## What you need

- The company's mail domains, for example `example.ch`.
- For each domain: the provider where its mail is, or the IMAP server the provider names. The box connects with TLS on port 993 and checks the server's certificate.

## Steps

### Add a mail domain

1. Open **Settings**, press **Advanced (for experts)**, then go to **Connections**, panel **Company mail**.
2. Type the domain under **Mail domain**, for example `example.ch`. For a second domain press **Add a domain** first.
3. Choose the provider under **Mail provider**: **Hostpoint**, **Infomaniak**, **cyon**, **METANET**, **green.ch**, **Swisscom (bluewin)**, **Gmail or Google Workspace (IMAP, app password)**, or **Another provider**.
4. Check **Incoming mail server (IMAP)**. A provider fills it in. For **Another provider**, type the server your provider names for IMAP.
5. Optional: **Webmail address (optional)** and **Drafts folder address (optional)**. With them, people get an **Open your drafts** button.
6. Press **Save**.

Each domain has its own mail server. A company with two domains at two providers adds two rows.

### Google Workspace

1. Add the domain and choose **Gmail or Google Workspace (IMAP, app password)**. Press **Save**.
2. Each person turns on 2-Step Verification in their Google account and creates an app password at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords.
3. Each person connects on **Your account** › **Email inbox**: the server appears by itself; they paste the app password and press **Test and connect**.

If your Google Workspace administrator switched app passwords off, the test fails with Google's answer. Switch them on in the Google Admin console.

### Company mailboxes by address

For a company without its own domain, for example a Gmail address as the company's mail.

1. In the same panel, under **Company mailboxes by address**, press **Add a company mailbox by address**.
2. Type the **Address**.
3. Choose the person under **Belongs to**.
4. Press **Save**.

Free-mail domains such as gmail.com are never company domains: if gmail.com were one, every person's private Gmail would count as company mail. List each company address at a free-mail provider one by one instead. "Gmail, GMX, iCloud, Yahoo and bluewin addresses" can be listed this way.

### Change a domain's mail server

1. Choose another **Mail provider** or type another **Incoming mail server (IMAP)** on the domain's row.
2. Press **Save**.

"Changing a domain's server disconnects the mailboxes in that domain; their owners see "Connect again" on Home." Each person connects again with their password.

### Remove a domain or an address

Press **Remove domain** or **Remove address** on the row, then **Save**.

## What you see afterwards

People connect their own company mailbox on **Your account** › **Email inbox**: see [Connect your company mailbox](/use/email/). Only an address in one of your domains, or on your list, can be connected as a company mailbox. Any other address is the person's one private mailbox: see [Add a private mailbox](/use/private-mailbox/).

You never see a person's mail, nor the address or the mail of a private mailbox. **Settings** › **Plan and costs** shows one line for all private mailboxes together, for example "Private mailboxes: CHF 1.20 this month".

## The signature samples

The tick box **Collect signature samples for the company standard** is on by default. It means: "only tasks the administrator starts; only the closing lines of the last 5 sent mails of your own mailbox, and of each person who said yes to sharing theirs". Untick it and press **Save** to stop.

## If it does not work

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "... is a free-mail provider, never a company domain. Add the company's addresses there one by one instead." | Remove the domain row. Use **Add a company mailbox by address** for each company address. |
| People see "The administrator has not set the company's mail domains yet, so no company mailbox can be connected." | Add at least one domain or one address, then **Save**. |
| A person's test fails | Check the server with your provider. For Gmail or Google Workspace: app passwords must be allowed. |
| "For safety, passwords, keys and other secrets are entered only in the office." | Change the settings in the office. |

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Add a domain** | One more company mail domain with its own server. | The company uses several domains. | Yes: **Remove domain**. |
| **Add a company mailbox by address** | One company address at a free-mail provider, with its person. | The company has no own domain. | Yes: **Remove address**. |
| Another **Mail provider** or server | Changes where the box reads that domain's mail. | You moved your mail to another provider. | Yes, but people connect again each time. |
| **Collect signature samples for the company standard** | Lets your tasks read signature closing lines. | You want one company signature. | Yes: untick it. |
