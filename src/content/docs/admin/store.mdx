The store is the publisher's catalog of workers for your box. You pay one monthly price per [function](/start/glossary/#function). Payment happens on the store's payment page in your browser. The box never sees card data.

The store explains buying, billing and cancelling in its own documentation:

- [How buying works](https://faivr.ai/docs/how-buying-works)
- [Billing](https://faivr.ai/docs/billing)
- [Cancel](https://faivr.ai/docs/cancel)

## Who can do this

The administrator.

## Before you start

The box is enrolled. See [Store enrolment](/setup/store-enrolment/).

## Steps

### See the catalog

1. Open **Administration**, sub-tab **Packages**, panel **Store**.
2. Press **Refresh catalog**.
3. Each function shows its name, its monthly price, a badge with its state, and its workers.

### Subscribe

1. Press **Subscribe** on the function. ("Subscribe (no price yet)" cannot be pressed: the store has no price for it yet.)
2. Press **Open the payment page**. Pay on the store's page.
3. Come back to the box: "The store opens the payment page in your browser; come back here when it says the payment is received." The page checks every few seconds.
4. The badge changes from "checkout pending" to "active".

### Install a store worker

1. Under the function, open **Permissions** on the worker: **Requires**, **Will be granted**, **Declined**, **You choose at install**.
2. If the publisher shows "NOT trusted on this appliance yet", register it first under [Trusted publishers](/admin/packages/#trusted-publishers).
3. Answer the install questions.
4. Press **Install**.
5. Wait for the steps "Reserve the installation at the store", "Download the signed package", "Verify, unpack and bind the package", "Acknowledge the activation to the store".

The worker then appears under **Installed packages** marked "from the store". The store row says "Installed: manage it under Installed packages below."

### An install the store still holds

An install that failed and was removed can still be held by the store. The worker then shows **Install** with the note "The store still holds an install from this box that never finished. Install takes it over with the answers above." Answer the questions and press **Install**. The row under **Installed packages** then says "took over the store's unfinished install".

### Manage a subscription

1. On an active function, press **Manage subscription**.
2. Press **Open the subscription page**. It opens the store's page for invoices, payment method and cancellation. "The link is valid for a short time."

### Cancel a subscription

1. First uninstall every worker of the function (see [below](#what-happens-to-an-installed-worker)). **Cancel subscription** stays greyed out while one is installed.
2. Press **Cancel subscription**, then **Confirm cancel**.
3. "It ends at the period end." The badge shows "cancels" and the date.

## What you see afterwards

The badge of each function:

| Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| not subscribed | No subscription. **Subscribe** is offered. |
| checkout pending | Payment started, not received yet. |
| active | Paid. You can install the workers. |
| cancels and a date | Ends at the period end. The function says "Cancelled: the functions of this bundle can be used until ...". |
| payment past due | The store did not receive the payment. Use **Manage subscription**. |
| subscription ended | Ended. **Subscribe** is offered again. |

## What happens to an installed worker

- **Uninstall** a store worker: every binding is reversed, the worker's workspace on the box is deleted, and the signed receipt goes to the store. "The last package of a bundle ends its subscription at the period end."
- A cancelled subscription keeps its workers working until the period end. Then, within a day, they stop: they take no new tasks, **Chat** says "The subscription for ... has ended. Subscribe again to use this worker.", and the row under **Installed packages** says "Stopped" and "Files are kept until you uninstall." Nothing is deleted.
- **Subscribe** again to the function: the same workers resume with their files. Nothing is installed anew.
- Uninstall stays your decision; the box never uninstalls a worker by itself.
- Your tasks, files and knowledge stay on the box. They never went to the store.
- If the store cannot be reached, the box keeps the receipt and sends it later.

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Subscribe** | Starts a monthly subscription. | You want the function's workers. | Yes: cancel. |
| **Install** | Installs one worker of the function. | After subscribing. | Yes: **Uninstall** (the workspace is deleted). |
| **Manage subscription** | Opens the store's page for invoices and payment method. | Billing questions. | n/a |
| **Cancel subscription** | Ends the function at the period end. | No worker of it is installed and you do not need it. | Subscribe again. |

<NotYet>
A store worker can be installed only once per box, a store worker cannot be updated from the box, and the enrolment cannot be undone in the portal. For questions about prices and invoices, use **Manage subscription** or the store's documentation above.
</NotYet>
