The store is the publisher's catalog of agents for your box. You pay one monthly price per [function](/start/glossary/#function). A function is one or more agents for one job. Payment happens on the store's payment page in your browser. The box never sees card data.

An administrator opens the store with the button **Store** at the top of the page **Agents**. The page then says "Agents you can add, with their price per month." **Back to the agents** returns to the cards. Every function is one card: the functions you subscribed to come first, then the others. A card shows the function's picture, its name, its price per month, one line on what it does, and its agents.

The store connection, each install in detail and the trusted publishers are in **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Agents**. **Advanced** is the link **Advanced (for experts)** at the bottom of **Settings**.

The store explains buying, billing and cancelling in its own documentation:

- [How buying works](https://faivr.ai/docs/how-buying-works)
- [Billing](https://faivr.ai/docs/billing)
- [Cancel](https://faivr.ai/docs/cancel)

## Who can do this

The administrator. Members see the agents on the page **Agents** and ask for one with its card's button. See [Your agents](/admin/packages/#your-agents).

## What you need

The box is connected to the store. The activation code connects it. See [Store enrolment](/setup/store-enrolment/). If it is not connected, the Store says "This box is not connected to the store yet. The activation code connects it; support can help under Advanced." **Connect to the store** opens **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Agents**, **Store connection**.

## Steps

### See the catalog

1. Open **Agents** and press **Store**.
2. Read the cards. The catalog refreshes by itself when you open **Agents**. To fetch it now, press **Refresh catalog** in **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Agents**, **Store connection**.
3. A function you have not subscribed to shows **Subscribe** and its agents.
4. Under each agent, open "What it may do: ...". The summary counts the permissions, for example "What it may do: ... granted, ... not allowed by your rules, ... chosen at install". The list has one line per permission, with a mark:
   - "granted": your owner policy allows it;
   - "not allowed by your rules": your owner policy does not allow it, so it would be declined;
   - "you choose at install": an install question decides it (a website, a repository or a product).

A permission that hands work to another agent names that agent, for example "hand work to" and the agent's name. If that agent is not installed on your box, the line adds "(not installed here)".

If the store offers nothing yet, the Store says "Your store account has no agents yet. The publisher decides which agents your account sees; when they offer one, press Refresh catalog." If the store cannot be reached, it says "The store could not be reached. Check that this box is online, then try again." Press **Try again**.

### The publisher is not trusted here yet

The store's agents are signed by their publisher. Until the box trusts that publisher, a line above the cards says "The store's agents come from ... Allow agents from ... on this box so you can add them." Its agents cannot be installed until you allow it.

1. Open **Agents** and press **Store**.
2. Press **Allow agents from** and the publisher's name, above the cards.

This works only in the office, on the local address. The same choice, with the key's fingerprint, is in **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Agents**, **Store connection**: **Trust** and the publisher's name, then **Confirm trust**. To undo it, remove the publisher under **Trusted publishers**. More: [Trusted publishers](/admin/packages/#trusted-publishers).

### Subscribe

1. In **Store**, press **Subscribe** on the function's card. ("Subscribe (no price yet)" cannot be pressed: "There is no price for it yet.")
2. Press **Open the payment page**. Pay on the store's page.
3. Come back to the box: "The store opens the payment page in your browser; come back here when it says the payment is received." The page checks every few seconds.
4. After the payment the card lists its agents, each with **Install**.

If you do not pay within a day, the card says "The payment was not completed within a day. Subscribe again to open a new payment page."

### Install a store agent

A subscribed function's card lists each of its agents. An agent that is not installed shows "Not installed yet", "What it may do:" with its marks, its questions and **Install** right there.

1. Open **Agents**, press **Store**, and find the function's card.
2. Read "What it may do:" under the agent.
3. If the note next to **Install** asks you to press **Allow agents from** and the publisher's name first, do that. See [The publisher is not trusted here yet](#the-publisher-is-not-trusted-here-yet).
4. Answer the install questions under "This agent asks you to choose:".
5. Press **Install**.
6. Wait for the steps "Reserve the installation at the store", "Download the signed package", "Verify, unpack and bind the package", "Acknowledge the activation to the store".
7. If the box asks for a product mapping, choose a product for each name and press **Resume the install**.

The agent then says "Installed". When every agent of the function is installed, the card says "Installed". The agent now has its card on **Agents**, where you choose **Who may use it**. Each install in detail: [Installed agents in detail](/admin/packages/#installed-agents-in-detail).

### Install all agents of a function

A function with two or more agents not installed yet offers one form for all of them: "Install all ... agents".

1. Answer the questions. "Each question is asked once, also when several agents need it." A shared question, for example the repository or the product, names the agents that ask it.
2. Press **Install all**.
3. The box installs the agents one after the other.

Each agent can still be changed or uninstalled on its own afterwards. To install only one of them, open "Install only this agent" under it.

### An install the store still holds

An install that failed and was removed can still be held by the store. The agent then shows "Not installed here yet: the store still holds an install from this box that never finished." Its **Install** has the note "Install takes it over with the answers above." Answer the questions and press **Install**. The new install takes the held one over and says "Took over the store's unfinished install."

### Update a store agent

When the store has a newer version, the agent's card on **Agents** says "Update available" and the version, and so does the function's card in **Store**. Press **Update**. If the new version asks for a new permission, you read it first and decide: "This update asks for a new permission. You see it and decide before anything changes." See [Update](/admin/packages/#update).

### Manage a subscription

1. In **Store**, on the function's card, open **Subscription** and press **Manage subscription**.
2. Press **Open the subscription page**. "Invoices, payment method and cancellation live there. The link is valid for a short time."

### Cancel a subscription

1. First remove every agent of the function: press **Remove from this box** on its card on **Agents** (all agents of the function go together), or **Uninstall** each one in **Installed agents in detail**. See [below](#what-happens-to-an-installed-agent). While one is installed, **Cancel subscription** stays greyed out and the card says "To cancel, uninstall its agents first."
2. On the function's card, open **Subscription**, press **Cancel subscription**, then **Confirm cancel**.
3. It ends at the period end. The card says "Cancelled: its agents can be used until ..." and the date.

## What you see afterwards

In **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Agents**, panel **Installed agents in detail**, each function has a badge:

| Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| not subscribed | No subscription. The function's card in **Store** offers **Subscribe**. |
| checkout pending | Payment started, not received yet. The card offers **Open the payment page**. |
| active | Paid. You can install its agents. |
| cancels and a date | Ends at the period end. The card says "Cancelled: its agents can be used until ..." |
| payment past due | The store did not receive the payment. Use **Manage subscription**. |
| subscription ended | Ended. **Subscribe** is offered again. |

The line on a function's card in **Installed agents in detail**:

| Line | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| "Subscribed, not installed yet" | Paid, no agent installed. Install it on the store card. |
| "Subscribed · ..." and "installed" (one agent) | Paid, the one agent is installed. |
| "Subscribed · ..." and "all ... agents installed" | Paid, every agent is installed. |
| "Subscribed · ..." and "... of ... agents installed" | Paid, some agents are not installed yet. |
| "The subscription has ended. The installs below are stopped: subscribe again to use them, or uninstall them." | The subscription ended while agents were installed. |

When a subscription has ended and none of its agents is installed, the function is offered with **Subscribe** again.

## What happens to an installed agent

- **Uninstall** a store agent: every binding is reversed, the agent's workspace on the box is deleted, and the signed receipt goes to the store. The confirmation says: "the last agent of a function ends its subscription at the period end." The agent then leaves the list. It stays in the history.
- A cancelled subscription keeps its agents working until the period end. Then, within a day, they stop. They take no new tasks. **Chat** says "The subscription for ... has ended. Subscribe again to use this agent." The install in **Installed agents in detail** shows "Stopped: the subscription ended" and "Files are kept until you uninstall." Nothing is deleted.
- **Subscribe** again to the function: the same agents resume with their files. Nothing is installed anew.
- Uninstall stays your decision. The box never uninstalls an agent by itself.
- Your tasks, files and knowledge stay on the box. They never went to the store.
- If the store cannot be reached, the box keeps the receipt and sends it later.

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Allow agents from** the publisher | Trusts the store's publisher on this box. | Before the first install from the store. | Yes: **Remove** under **Trusted publishers**. |
| **Subscribe** | Starts a monthly subscription. | You want the function's agents. | Yes: cancel. |
| **Install** | Installs one agent of the function. | After subscribing. | Yes: **Uninstall** (the workspace is deleted). |
| **Install all** | Installs every agent of the function that is not installed yet. Shared questions are asked once. | You want the whole function. | Yes: **Remove from this box**, or **Uninstall** each agent. |
| **Update** | Moves the agent to the store's newer version. A new permission asks first. | "Update available" on a card. | No direct way back. |
| **Manage subscription** | Opens the store's page for invoices and payment method. | Billing questions. | n/a |
| **Cancel subscription** | Ends the function at the period end. | No agent of it is installed and you do not need it. | Subscribe again. |
