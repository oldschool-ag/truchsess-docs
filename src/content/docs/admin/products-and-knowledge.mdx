A [product](/start/glossary/#product) is a part of your company that work belongs to. Products decide which members see what, and which [knowledge](/start/glossary/#knowledge) a worker may read.

## Who can do this

- The administrator adds products, decides requests and imports knowledge.
- Members request products and agents on the tab **Products**, and see their requests on the tab **Requests**.

## Before you start

- A product id: lowercase letters, digits and hyphens, for example `design-system`. A worker refers to a product by this id.
- For an import: the documents as `.md` or `.txt` files, or one `.zip` with them. You must be in the office on **https://myai.local/**.

## Steps

### Add a product

1. Open the tab **Products**, panel **Add a product**.
2. Enter **Product id** and **Product name**.
3. Press **Add product**. The product is active at once.

### Decide a request

Members send requests on the tab **Products**: **Request a new product** and **Request another agent**.

1. Open **Administration**, sub-tab **General**, panel **Approvals**.
2. Read **Requested by**, **Request**, **Product** and **Cost / month**. "Approving a request authorizes its stated monthly cost."
3. Press **Approve** or **Deny**, adjust the reason, and press the button again to confirm.

An approved product request makes the product active, and the requester becomes its owner. An approved agent request records the agent under the product. It does not install a worker.

### Import documents

1. Open **Administration**, sub-tab **Knowledge**.
2. Choose the **Product**.
3. Choose the **Files**: one `.zip`, or several `.md` and `.txt` files.
4. Press **Upload and review**.
5. Read the review list: **Key**, **Original name**, **Title**, **Size**, **Status** ("new", "changed: ... line(s) added, ... removed", or "unchanged"). Files that cannot be imported are listed under **Skipped** with the reason.
6. Tick or untick documents under **Import**. Unchanged documents are not selected.
7. Press **Import selected**, or **Discard** to stop.

## What you see afterwards

- The new product appears on **Products** and in every product list of the portal.
- After an import: "Imported N document(s) into ... Earlier versions of changed documents stay readable." Workers on that product read the documents in their next task. See [Knowledge](/use/knowledge/).

### Older revisions

Each import of a changed document makes a new revision. The earlier revisions stay in the box, with who wrote them and when. A worker with knowledge permission can read an older revision on request.

To go back to an earlier text, import the earlier file again: it shows as "changed", and **Import selected** makes it the current version.

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Add product** | Creates an active product. | A new area of work. | No: a product cannot be deleted or renamed. |
| **Approve** a product request | Makes the product active, the requester its owner. | The cost and purpose are fine. | No. |
| **Deny** | Rejects the request. | | No. |
| **Import selected** | Writes the chosen documents. | After reading the review. | Partly: import the earlier file again. A new document cannot be removed. |
| **Discard** | Throws the upload away. Nothing is written. | Wrong files. | n/a |

Limits of an import: at most 50 MB and 2,000 files per upload, at most 64 KB per document, text in UTF-8 only. An upload is deleted after an hour, or when you leave the page.

Skipped files and why: "not a text document" (not `.md` or `.txt`), "binary", "too large" (over 64 KB), "bad name", "link", "path with ..", "same key as another file", "more than 2,000 files".

<NotYet>
You cannot delete or rename a product, withdraw a request, see the knowledge documents or their revisions in the portal, or delete a document. An approved agent request does not create a worker; install a package for that. Ask a worker with knowledge permission to read or quote documents for you.
