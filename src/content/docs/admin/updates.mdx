Updates are on **Administration** › **System**, panel **System**. "Every change on this appliance comes from a signed release the box verifies before switching. No files are edited by hand."

The same page holds the [company name](#the-company-name), **Voice input**, the **Self-tests** (see [Audit and status](/admin/audit-and-status/)) and **Earlier assistant conversations**.

## Who can do this

The administrator, in the office on **https://truchsess.local/**. The company name can also be changed on the remote address.

## Before you start

- No task is running. The AI part of the box restarts during an update, and running tasks may fail.
- Recommended: press **Back up now** on **Administration** › **Backups**.

## Steps

### Update

1. Open **Administration**, then **System**, panel **System**.
2. Press **Check for updates**.
3. **Newest available** shows the newest release. The button now reads **Update now to** and the release name.
4. Press it, then **Confirm update** within 10 seconds.
5. The box checks once more for the newest release. If a newer one appeared meanwhile, nothing is installed and the button offers the newer one. Press it again.
6. Follow the steps: "Verify the release signature", "Download the system closure", "Verify the closure (sha256 and signature)", "Import the closure into the Nix store", "Switch to the new system generation", "Restart the assistant runtime", "Self-check (services and health endpoints)", "Record the release and prune old generations".
7. During the switch the page shows "Portal restarting, reconnecting". Wait. It continues by itself.

### Cancel an update

**Cancel update** is shown while the update downloads and checks. Press it, then **Confirm stop**. "It stops before switching: nothing changes on the appliance, and what was downloaded is kept for the next Update now." After the switch has started, an update cannot be cancelled.

### Roll back

1. Press **Roll back**, then **Confirm roll back**.
2. The box switches to the previous version. The portal and the AI part restart.

**Roll back** is greyed out when there is no previous version, for example on a box that was never updated.

## What you see afterwards

- **Running release** shows the new release.
- The outcome line, for example "Now running ..., generation ..., took ... min ... s".
- "reboot pending (new kernel)": the update is active, but part of it needs a restart of the computer. Restart the box when convenient.

## What happens by itself

- The box looks for a new release once a day. It never installs it by itself.
- When a new release is ready, **Administration** › **Overview** lists "Version ... is ready to install." under **Needs your attention**.
- If the switch fails, or the self-check after the switch fails (it waits up to 7 minutes), the box returns to the previous version by itself.
- The box keeps the three newest versions.
- The box never restarts the computer during an update.
- A slow download is retried. If it still fails: "The download from GitHub is too slow or stopped. Nothing was changed. Try again later."
- An older release is never offered: "not offered: older than the running release".

## The company name

The panel **Company name** is the first panel on **Administration** › **System**. The name is "Shown in the navigation and on the sign-in page."

1. Open **Administration**, then **System**, panel **Company name**.
2. Type the name in **Company name** (at most 80 characters).
3. Press **Save**. The panel shows "Saved."

Afterwards:

- Everyone sees the name in the navigation, under their own name and after their role.
- The sign-in page says "Sign in to the Truchsess box of" and the name.
- **Administration** › **Overview**, **Recent activity** records the change.

The name may stay empty. Until it is set, the navigation shows the role alone. To remove the name, empty the field and press **Save**: "The company name is cleared."

### After the update that brings the new address

The office address is now **https://truchsess.local/**; `myai.local` keeps working. The outcome line of that update ends with "The office address is now truchsess.local; myai.local still works. Your browser will warn once more about the box's certificate."

- The box renewed its certificate once, to cover both names. Each browser warns once more: compare the fingerprint with "Certificate fingerprint of this Truchsess" on the box's screen or in **Administration** › **Remote access**, then accept.
- Sign in once more on `truchsess.local`. A password saved in the browser for `myai.local` may need to be typed once.

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Check for updates** | Looks for the newest release. | Any time. | n/a |
| **Update now** | Installs the newest release. | When no task is running. | Yes: **Roll back**. |
| **Cancel update** | Stops before the switch. | You started at a bad time. | Press **Update now** again later. |
| **Roll back** | Returns to the previous version. | The new version causes a problem. | Yes: update again. |
| **Save** (company name) | Shows the name in the navigation and on the sign-in page. | Once, at setup. | Yes: change it or empty it. |

If the page shows "The session ended while the update ran; sign in again to see the result.", sign in again. If it shows "The portal has not answered for 15 minutes", reload the page and look at **Details (update log)**.

<NotYet>
Updates do not install themselves, and you cannot choose an older release. To go back, use **Roll back** (one step back). For anything else, ask support. What changed in each release is listed on [Releases](/releases/).
</NotYet>
