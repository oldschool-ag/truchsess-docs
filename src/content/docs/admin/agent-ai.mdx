Every agent works on the box's level (Cheap, Standard or Frontier, see [model lanes](/admin/ai-providers-and-lanes/#model-lanes)) unless an administrator names an AI model for it. You can give one agent its own AI, for example a stronger model for a reviewer, and up to three other AIs it may ask for a short second opinion.

## Who can do this

- The administrator chooses an agent's AI and its second opinions.
- Everyone sees which AI an agent works on. Members see the line, not the choice.

## What you need

- The agent is installed. See [Install and manage agents](/admin/packages/).
- For a model of your ChatGPT plan: the box is signed in with ChatGPT. See [Use a ChatGPT subscription](/admin/chatgpt-subscription/).

## Steps

### Give an agent its own AI

1. Open **Agents**, click the agent's card, and go to **For administrators**, part **AI for this agent**. "Every agent works on the box's level unless you name an AI model for it here. The box checks your data rule when you save and before every task." A function of several agents, such as Bob, has one block for each of its agents.
2. Under the agent's name, choose the model. The list has the default first, for example **The box's level (Standard: Kimi K2.6)**, then **Your ChatGPT plan** (while the box is signed in with ChatGPT) and **The box's AI**.
3. For a model that reasons, choose **Normal reasoning**, **High reasoning** or **Highest reasoning**.
4. Press **Save**. The box says, for example, "Bob - QA Reviewer now works on GPT-6 Astra on your ChatGPT plan, highest reasoning. From its next task on."

To go back to the box's level, choose **The box's level (...)** and press **Save**.

### Give an agent second opinions

In the same block: "Second opinions: up to 3 other AIs this agent may ask for a short second opinion. They get only the text the agent sends: no tools, no memory, no files."

1. In rows **1.** to **3.**, choose a model, or keep **No second opinion**. For a model that reasons, choose its reasoning.
2. Press **Save**. "... It may ask for 2 second opinions. From its next task on."

When the agent asks for a second opinion:

- The other AI gets only the text the agent sends: no tools, no memory, no files.
- Its answer comes back marked as information from another model, not as instructions.
- The box checks your data rule for each second opinion, charges its cost to the agent that asked, and records which AI answered.

## The data rule

A model that does not meet your [data rule](/start/glossary/#data-rule) for this agent is not in the list. The box checks it again before every task. When the rule keeps your ChatGPT plan out, one line says why: "Your ChatGPT plan is not offered: ...".

## New models from OpenRouter

Since release 2026.10.25-af84ac2, with OpenRouter as the box's AI (your own key or the AI that came with the box):

- **AI for this agent**, its second opinions and the model lanes list the models OpenRouter serves now, grouped by company, for example "Anthropic on OpenRouter". A model OpenRouter adds shows within a few hours, without an update. Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6.1 Sol are among them.
- A model is listed only when one of its providers is known to meet your data rule.
- **A short list:** per company the 3 newest models from the last half year, plus any model an agent or a lane already uses.
- **Find a model:** with a long list, type a name (for example "Opus") to narrow the lists. The model you chose stays visible.
- **An OpenRouter account that requires zero retention:** do not switch the box's rule to "never kept, never trained on" just for that: the rule also turns off your ChatGPT plan's models and any lane on the plan. Under "never trained on", a model only OpenRouter's list names is offered only with a provider that keeps nothing.

## What you see afterwards

- **Cards:** every agent's card on **Agents** and on **Home** has one grey line with its AI, for example "AI: GPT-5.6 Sol on your ChatGPT plan, highest reasoning" or "AI: the box's level (Standard: Kimi K2.6)". A function whose roles work on different AIs says "AI: different for its roles (see its settings)".
- **For members:** the agent's settings show the part **Its AI**, with the same line.
- **Chat:** the header names the agent's lane and the AI behind it, for example "Frontier lane: GPT-6 Astra on your ChatGPT plan", or for an agent with its own AI "Own AI: GPT-5.6 Sol on your ChatGPT plan, highest reasoning".
- **A task's Details:** the row **Models** names the model each step ran on, as the AI reported it, for example `openai/gpt-6-astra`, even after the lane changed. A task from before this update may say "not recorded".
- **Costs:** tasks on your ChatGPT plan cost nothing on the box's budget. **Settings** › **Advanced** › **AI**, panel **Costs in detail**, shows under the agent, for example, "12 runs on your ChatGPT plan, 3 second opinions" and "nothing on this budget". See [Budget](/admin/budget/).

## If it does not work

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The agent's card: "Problem: its AI is not available: the ChatGPT plan is signed out" | Sign in with ChatGPT again under **Settings** › **Advanced** › **AI** (administrators: **Open AI settings** on the card), or choose another AI for the agent. The agent never uses another AI on its own. |
| A message to the agent: "Its AI is not available: the ChatGPT plan is signed out. Sign in with ChatGPT again under Settings > Advanced > AI, or choose another AI for this agent. Nothing ran, so nothing was charged." | The same. Then press **Try again** under the task. |
| "Your ChatGPT plan's usage limit is reached. It resets at ..." | Wait until the time it names. The task is not retried and not moved to another AI. |
| A second opinion: "The OpenAI review did not run (plan limit), try again after ..." | The agent says the review did not run. Ask again after that time. |
| "... is not on the box's list of models." | Choose a model from the list. |

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The box's level (...)** | The agent works on its lane, like every other agent. | Default. | n/a |
| A model under **Your ChatGPT plan** or **The box's AI** | The agent works on that model, from its next task. | A job that needs a stronger or a different AI. | Yes: choose the box's level again. |
| **Normal reasoning**, **High reasoning**, **Highest reasoning** | How long a reasoning model thinks. Higher costs more, or uses more of your ChatGPT plan. | Reviews and hard questions: higher. | Yes. |
| Second opinions **1.** to **3.** | Other AIs the agent may ask, with text only. | A reviewer that should check its findings with another AI company. | Yes: **No second opinion**. |

<NotYet>
An agent never moves to another AI by itself when its AI is not available: the task stops with a plain line. The box cannot say which reasoning the AI really used; it records the model the AI reports.
</NotYet>
