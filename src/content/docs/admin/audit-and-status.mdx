The records are for experts. They are on **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Activity and audit trail** ("Every task of every agent, the audit trail of one task, sign-ins and changes, and the older requests.") and **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Box**. Advanced is the link **Advanced (for experts)** at the bottom of Settings.

## Who can do this

The administrator. Members see their own recent tasks on each agent's card under **Agents**, and on Home.

## What you need

Nothing. Everything here only reads.

## At a glance: Home

There is no Overview page any more. What waits for you is on Home, **Needs you**: "Only a person can unblock these." Administrators also see Home, **Box** ("Only administrators see this."), with the tiles **Last safety copy**, **Update**, **AI costs this month** and **Reach your box from home**, and the link **Open Settings**.

## Where things are recorded

| Place | What it shows | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Activity and audit trail**, panel **Run history** | Every task given to an agent: state, timing, model lane, approvals used, result files. Filter by **Agent**, or with **All**, **Needs you**, **Active**, **Finished**, **Failed or cancelled**, **Routines**, and **Search tasks**. | Administrator |
| The same, **Audit** on a task, panel **Audit trail** | Every event of one task: when it started, each step, each approval decision with the name, each knowledge or workboard call, cancel, failure. Columns **Time**, **Event**, **Actor**, **Summary**. | Administrator |
| The same, panel **Recent activity** | The last 200 events of the box: sign-ins, accounts, product access, requests, approvals, work given to agents, agent installs, provider changes, the company name. Columns **Time**, **Person**, **Activity**. | Administrator |
| The same, panel **Requests** | Older requests from members for a new product or another agent. | Administrator |
| **Settings** › **Advanced** › **AI**, panel **Costs in detail**, **Budget events** | Warnings, stops, refused runs, top-up requests. | Administrator |
| **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Box**, panel **Version and log**, **Backup history** | Safety copies, key changes, restores. | Administrator |
| **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Connections**, panel **Recent changes** | "Who connected, replaced or removed what, and when. Never a secret." | Administrator |
| **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Agent rules**, panel **Owner policy** | "Last change ... by ..." | Administrator |
| **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Box**, panel **Old assistant conversations** | Who wrote how many messages to the removed built-in assistant. You cannot read them: only the person who wrote them opens or downloads them, under **Your account**. You can delete one person's messages (**Delete ...'s messages**) or **Delete everyone's messages**, with **Undo** for 15 minutes. | Administrator |

## Status

### Steps

1. The status line at the bottom of the menu shows the AI provider and model when the box is ready ("Ready"), or "Starting" while it starts.
2. Home: **Needs you**, and for administrators **Box**.
3. **Settings** › **Updates**: **On this box** and **Newest**. See [Updates](/admin/updates/).
4. **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Box**, panel **Version and log**: the exact release, the update log under **Details (update log)**, and **Updates at night**.
5. **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Agents**: the line above **Store connection** says what the box's helper did last ("Last request: ...").
6. **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Agents**, panel **Installed agents in detail**: the state of each agent, and **Ready** or what it needs.
7. **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Box**, panel **Self-tests**: press **Run the sandbox tests** or **Run the Connection secrecy test**. Each shows its progress and the result in the panel.

## What you see afterwards

A self-test shows its result in the panel **Self-tests**. The last run of each test stays visible. To see the test tasks in **Run history**, tick **Show self-tests**.

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Run the sandbox tests** | Checks that agents stay inside their closed workspace. | After an update, or when support asks. | n/a |
| **Run the Connection secrecy test** | Checks that agents cannot read any key. | After a connection change. | n/a |
| **Run the gateway test** | "The gateway test restarts the assistant runtime twice and takes several minutes. Chat is unavailable meanwhile." Press it, then **Confirm gateway test**. Refused while a chat run is active. | Only when support asks. | n/a |
| **Show self-tests** (Run history) | Shows the test tasks in the run history. | When you look at a self-test. | Yes. |

<NotYet>
You cannot export the activity or the audit trail, and the box keeps only the last 200 events in **Recent activity**. Copy what you need. **Search tasks** searches the run history only. The box sends no alerts.
</NotYet>
