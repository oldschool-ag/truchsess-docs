## Who can do this

The administrator, in the office on **https://myai.local/**. Creating accounts is refused on the remote address.

## Before you start

- The person's name and work e-mail. One e-mail can have only one account.
- The product the person should work on. See [Products and knowledge](/admin/products-and-knowledge/).

## Steps

### Create an account

1. Open **Administration**, sub-tab **General**, panel **Add a user**.
2. Enter **Name** and **Work email**.
3. Press **Create user**.
4. Copy the temporary password from the line "Temporary password for ...". It is not shown again.
5. Give it to the person safely. At the first sign-in they choose their own password. See [Members](/setup/members/#the-members-first-sign-in).

### Give product access

1. In **Assign product access**, choose **User**, **Product** and **Access** (**Member** or **Owner**).
2. Press **Assign access**. The box shows "Product access assigned."

Assigning again with another **Access** changes the access.

### Remove product access

1. In the panel **Product access**, find the line.
2. Press **Remove**, then **Confirm remove**.

## What you see afterwards

- **People** lists every account: **Name**, **Email**, **Role**, **Status**, **Last login**. A new account shows "Temporary password" until the first sign-in.
- **Product access** lists every assignment: **Person**, **Product**, **Access**, **Assigned**.
- **Company activity** records each change with your name.

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Create user** | A new member account. | A new colleague. | No. |
| **Member** access | The person sees the product. | Default. | Yes. |
| **Owner** access | The same as Member in this release. Also given automatically to a person whose product request you approve. | The person is responsible for the product. | Yes. |
| **Remove** | Removes the person from the product. | The person leaves the product. | Yes: assign again. |

What a member sees does not depend only on product access: every member sees every installed worker in **Chat**, and their own runs and requests.

<NotYet>
You cannot delete or disable an account, rename it, change its e-mail, reset a forgotten password, or make a second administrator. Workarounds: remove all product access of a person who leaves; give a person who forgot their password a new account with another e-mail. A person whose account still exists can still sign in. Ask support to close an account.
</NotYet>
