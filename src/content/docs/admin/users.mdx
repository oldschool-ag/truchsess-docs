Everything about people is on **Settings** › **People**: "Everyone who can sign in to this box. A new person sees the company's agents at once." The section holds the table of people and **Add a person**.

Product access is for experts. It is on **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Knowledge**, panels **Assign product access** and **Product access**. Advanced is the link **Advanced (for experts)** at the bottom of Settings.

## Who can do this

The administrator. Some actions work only in the office on **https://truchsess.local/**; see [On the remote address](#on-the-remote-address).

## What you need

- The person's name and work e-mail. One e-mail can have only one account.
- For product access: the product. See [Products and knowledge](/admin/products-and-knowledge/).

## Steps

### Create an account

1. Open **Settings** › **People**, part **Add a person**.
2. Enter **Name** and **Work email**.
3. Press **Add the person**.
4. The box shows the one-time password: "One-time password for ... Copy it now; it is not shown again." Press **Copy**.
5. Give it to the person safely, with the box's address. At the first sign-in they choose their own password. See [Members](/setup/members/#the-members-first-sign-in).

### Give product access

1. Open **Settings**, press **Advanced (for experts)**, then go to **Knowledge**, panel **Assign product access**.
2. Choose **User**, **Product** and **Access** (**Member** or **Owner**).
3. Press **Assign access**. The box shows "Product access assigned."

Assigning again with another **Access** changes the access.

### Remove product access

1. On **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Knowledge**, in the panel **Product access**, find the line.
2. Press **Remove**, then **Confirm remove**.

### Reset a forgotten password

1. On **Settings** › **People**, press **Reset password** on the person's row.
2. Press **Confirm reset**. "Every session of this person ends now."
3. A window shows "New one-time password for ...". Press **Copy**. It is not shown again.
4. Press **Done**.
5. Give the password to the person safely. At the next sign-in they must choose a new password.

Every browser where the person is signed in is signed out at once. Failed sign-ins are cleared, so a person who was locked out for 15 minutes can sign in right away.

### Remove a person

1. On **Settings** › **People**, press **Remove** on the person's row.
2. Type the person's e-mail address in the field.
3. Press **Confirm remove**.

"Remove ... for good? They are signed out at once." The account can never sign in again, in the office or on the remote address. Its product access is removed, its personal memory is deleted, and its e-mail address is free for a new account. Nothing in the history is deleted: chat lines, requests, approvals and activity keep the name, with "(removed person)" next to it.

### Enable a person again

There is no **Disable** button any more. **Enable** shows only on the row of a person an older version of the box disabled.

1. Press **Enable** on that person's row.
2. Press **Confirm enable**. "... can sign in again, with the same password."

### Change a name or e-mail address

1. Press **Change name or e-mail** on the person's row.
2. Change the name or the e-mail address.
3. Press **Save**.

"A new e-mail address signs the person out; they sign in with the new one." If the person uses the [remote address](/admin/remote-access/) and the e-mail code does not arrive at the new address, ask support.

## What you see afterwards

- **Settings** › **People** lists every account: **Name**, **Email**, **Role** (Administrator or Member), **State**, **Last login**, and the buttons.
- **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Knowledge**, panel **Product access** lists every assignment: **Person**, **Product**, **Access**, **Assigned**.
- **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Activity and audit trail**, panel **Recent activity** records each change with your name.

The **State** column:

| State | Meaning | Buttons |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Active | The person signs in with their own password. | **Reset password**, **Change name or e-mail**, **Remove** |
| Not signed in yet | A new account, or a password was reset. The person must choose a new password at the next sign-in. | **Reset password**, **Change name or e-mail**, **Remove** |
| Disabled | An older version of the box disabled the person. They cannot sign in. | **Change name or e-mail**, **Enable**, **Remove** |
| Removed | The account is closed for good. | None |

Your own row, the administrator's, has no buttons. Change your own password under [Your account](/use/your-account/): press the picture with your initials at the top right. If you forgot your own password, see [Administrator password](/admin/administrator-password/).

## On the remote address

Settings open on the remote address only while **Allow Settings on the remote address** is on (**Settings** › **Your company**, **Reach your box from home**). Then an administrator enters the box password once per session: Home, **Needs you**, field "Your box password", button **Manage the box**.

| Works on the remote address | Only in the office, on https://truchsess.local/ |
| --- | --- |
| **Remove** (it only takes access away) | **Add the person**, **Reset password**, **Enable**, **Change name or e-mail** |
| **Assign access**, **Remove** in **Product access** | |

In the office-only cases the box says "For safety, passwords, keys and other secrets are entered only in the office." and names the local address.

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Add the person** | A new member account. | A new colleague. | Yes: **Remove**. |
| **Member** access | The person sees the product. | Default. | Yes. |
| **Owner** access | The same as Member in this release. Also given automatically to a person whose product request you approve. | The person is responsible for the product. | Yes. |
| **Remove** (product access) | Removes the person from the product. | The person leaves the product. | Yes: assign again. |
| **Reset password** | A new one-time password; the person is signed out. | The person forgot the password. | No, but the person chooses a new one. |
| **Remove** (person) | Signs the person out and closes the account for good; the e-mail is free again. | The person left. | No. Add the person again: a new account. |
| **Change name or e-mail** | A new name or e-mail address. | A typo, a new name, a new address. | Yes: change it again. |

Which agents a person may use is set on each agent's card under **Agents**: **Who may use it**, **Everyone** or **These people**.

<NotYet>
You cannot make a second administrator or make a member an administrator, or change the administrator's own name or e-mail in the portal. Ask support. You cannot pause an account for a while: remove it, and add the person again when they come back.
</NotYet>
