Everything about people is on **Settings** › **People and access**, section **People**: "Everyone who can sign in to this box. A new person gets Truchsess and the agents you tick." The section holds the table of people and **Add a person**. The same page holds **Reach the box from home**, see [Remote access](/admin/remote-access/).

Product access is for experts. It is on **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Knowledge**, panels **Assign product access** and **Product access**. Advanced is the link **Advanced (for experts)** at the bottom of Settings.

## Who can do this

The administrator. Some actions work only in the office on **https://truchsess.local/**; see [On the remote address](#on-the-remote-address).

## What you need

- The person's name and work e-mail. One e-mail can have only one account.
- For product access: the product. See [Products and knowledge](/admin/products-and-knowledge/).

## Steps

### Add a person

1. Open **Settings** › **People and access**, part **Add a person**.
2. Enter **Name** and **Work e-mail**.
3. Keep **Member: uses the agents**. Choose **Administrator: also manages people, costs and the box** only if they should also manage people, costs and the box; then type **Your own password, to invite an administrator**.
4. Under "Which agents may ... use?", tick the agents. Truchsess is always ticked: "Included for everyone".
5. Press **Invite** and the first name, for example **Invite Anna**. "Anna is invited. Send the invitation now: the link works once, for 7 days."
6. Press **Send from my e-mail** (it opens your mail program with the invitation written) or **Copy the invitation** (paste it into an e-mail or a chat). "The box sends no e-mail."

With the link, the person sets a password only they know, in the office. See [The invitation page](/setup/members/#the-invitation-page).

Until the link is used, the person's row says "Invited (the link works until ...)". **Send again** makes a new link; the earlier one stops working. **Withdraw** stops the link: "The link stops working and ... cannot sign in. Send again invites ... anew."

The old way, a person with a one-time password, is for support only: **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Support access**, "Add a person with a one-time password (for support only)".

### Give product access

1. Open **Settings**, press **Advanced (for experts)**, then go to **Knowledge**, panel **Assign product access**.
2. Choose **User**, **Product** and **Access** (**Member** or **Owner**).
3. Press **Assign access**. The box shows "Product access assigned."

Assigning again with another **Access** changes the access.

### Remove product access

1. On **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Knowledge**, in the panel **Product access**, find the line.
2. Press **Remove**, then **Confirm remove**.

### Reset a forgotten password

1. On **Settings** › **People and access**, press **Reset password** on the person's row.
2. Press **Confirm reset**. "Every session of this person ends now."
3. A small window opens: "New one-time password for ...". The password is in its own field. Press **Copy**. The window says "Copied."
4. Press **Done**. The password is shown only once: copy it before you press **Done**.
5. Give the password to the person safely. At the next sign-in they must choose a new password.

Every browser where the person is signed in is signed out at once. Failed sign-ins are cleared, so a person who was locked out for 15 minutes can sign in right away.

### Remove a person

1. On **Settings** › **People and access**, press **Remove** on the person's row.
2. Type the person's e-mail address in the field.
3. Press **Confirm remove**.

"Remove ... for good? They are signed out at once." The account can never sign in again, in the office or on the remote address. Its product access is removed, its personal memory is deleted, and its e-mail address is free for a new account. Nothing in the history is deleted: chat lines, requests, approvals and activity keep the name, with "(removed person)" next to it.

### Enable a person again

There is no **Disable** button any more. **Enable** shows only on the row of a person an older version of the box disabled.

1. Press **Enable** on that person's row.
2. Press **Confirm enable**. "... can sign in again, with the same password."

### Change a name or e-mail address

1. Press **Change name or e-mail** on the person's row.
2. Change the name or the e-mail address.
3. Press **Save**.

"A new e-mail address signs the person out; they sign in with the new one." If the person uses the [remote address](/admin/remote-access/) and the e-mail code does not arrive at the new address, ask support.

## What you see afterwards

- **Settings** › **People and access** lists every account: **Name**, **Email**, **Role** (Administrator or Member), **State**, **Last login**, and the buttons.
- **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Knowledge**, panel **Product access** lists every assignment: **Person**, **Product**, **Access**, **Assigned**.
- **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Activity and audit trail**, panel **Recent activity** records each change with your name.

The **State** column:

| State | Meaning | Buttons |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Active | The person signs in with their own password. | **Reset password**, **Change name or e-mail**, **Remove** |
| Invited (the link works until ...) | The person has an invitation and has not set a password yet. | **Send again**, **Withdraw**, **Remove** |
| Invitation expired | The 7 days passed before the person used the link. | **Send again**, **Remove** |
| Invitation withdrawn | You withdrew the invitation. | **Send again**, **Remove** |
| Not signed in yet | A password was reset, or the person was added with a one-time password. The person must choose a new password at the next sign-in. | **Reset password**, **Change name or e-mail**, **Remove** |
| Disabled | An older version of the box disabled the person. They cannot sign in. | **Change name or e-mail**, **Enable**, **Remove** |
| Removed | The account is closed for good. | None |

Your own row, the administrator's, has no buttons. Change your own password under [Your account](/use/your-account/): press your initials in the menu (on a phone, at the top right). If you forgot your own password, see [Administrator password](/admin/administrator-password/).

## On the remote address

Settings open on the remote address only while **Allow Settings on the remote address** is on (**Settings** › **People and access**, **Reach the box from home**). Then an administrator enters the box password once per session: **Home**, **Needs you**, field "Your box password", button **Manage the box**. Until then the administrator works like a member there. See [Remote access](/admin/remote-access/#settings-on-the-remote-address-administrator).

| Works on the remote address | Only in the office, on https://truchsess.local/ |
| --- | --- |
| **Remove** (it only takes access away) | **Invite**, **Send again**, **Reset password**, **Enable**, **Change name or e-mail** |
| **Assign access**, **Remove** in **Product access** | |

In the office-only cases the box says "For safety, passwords, keys and other secrets are entered only in the office." and names the local address.

## If it does not work

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Select the password and copy it." | The browser did not let the page copy. Select the password in its field and copy it yourself, then press **Done**. |
| You pressed **Done** before you copied the password. | Press **Reset password** again: the box makes a new one-time password. |
| "The list could not be refreshed: ..." | The action itself worked. Reload the page to see the list. |
| "For safety, passwords, keys and other secrets are entered only in the office." | You are on the remote address. Do this in the office. |
| The person says the one-time password is refused. | Ask them to copy the whole password and nothing more, or to type it. |

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Invite** | A new person, with an invitation link for 7 days. | A new colleague. | Yes: **Withdraw**, or **Remove**. |
| **Send again** | A new invitation link; the earlier one stops working. | The person lost the link, or it expired. | n/a |
| **Withdraw** | Stops the invitation link. | You invited the wrong person, or too early. | Yes: **Send again**. |
| **Member** access | The person sees the product. | Default. | Yes. |
| **Owner** access | The same as Member in this release. Also given automatically to a person whose product request you approve. | The person is responsible for the product. | Yes. |
| **Remove** (product access) | Removes the person from the product. | The person leaves the product. | Yes: assign again. |
| **Reset password** | A new one-time password; the person is signed out. | The person forgot the password. | No, but the person chooses a new one. |
| **Remove** (person) | Signs the person out and closes the account for good; the e-mail is free again. | The person left. | No. Add the person again: a new account. |
| **Change name or e-mail** | A new name or e-mail address. | A typo, a new name, a new address. | Yes: change it again. |

Which agents a person may use is set on each agent's card under **Agents**: **Who may use it**, **Everyone** or **These people**.

<NotYet>
You cannot make a second administrator or make a member an administrator, or change the administrator's own name or e-mail in the portal. Ask support. You cannot pause an account for a while: remove it, and add the person again when they come back.
</NotYet>
