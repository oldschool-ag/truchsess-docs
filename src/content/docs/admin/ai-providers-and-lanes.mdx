## Who can do this

The administrator. Changing the provider works only in the office.

## Before you start

- To change the provider: the new provider's API key.
- Know the rule for your data: may a provider keep it or train on it, and where may it be processed?

## Change the AI provider

1. Open **Administration**, sub-tab **General**, panel **People and access**, and press **Change AI provider**.
2. Choose the **Billing mode**, the **Provider** and the **Model ID**, and enter the key.
3. Press **Save provider**. The box restarts its AI part: "Starting the assistant".
4. The model lanes now hold the new provider's default models. Go to [Model lanes](#model-lanes) and press **Apply to installed packages**.

The choices are explained on [AI providers](/setup/ai-providers/#your-choices).

## The data rule

The panel **Which AI providers may see your data** is on **Administration**, sub-tab **Packages**. "Every model request of every package goes only to providers that meet this rule." A run that cannot meet it is refused before any data leaves the box.

1. Under **What providers may do with your data**, choose one option.
2. Under **Where your data may be processed**, choose one option.
3. Press **Save data rule**.

| What providers may do | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Never kept, never trained on** (default) | "Only providers that neither keep your data nor train on it (zero data retention)." |
| **Never trained on** | "Only providers that never train on your data. They may keep it for a while, for example for abuse monitoring." |
| **Any provider, may keep and train on your data** | "Any provider. Some keep your data and train their models on it." |

| Where | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Any country** (default) | Providers in any country. |
| **EU or Switzerland** | Only PHOENIQS (Switzerland) or a model on this box. OpenRouter lanes are refused. |
| **Switzerland only** | Only PHOENIQS (Switzerland) or a model on this box. Every other lane is refused before any call. |
| **On this box only** | Only a model running on the box. "No local model ships yet: runs are refused." |

"Packages that read or write your code or knowledge, or use stored credentials, always run at least 'never trained on'."

## The Swiss-only option

To keep your data in Switzerland:

1. Get an API key from PHOENIQS.
2. Change the provider to **PHOENIQS (Switzerland)** with **Billing mode** **Own key** (see above).
3. Set the data rule's **Where your data may be processed** to **Switzerland only**, and save.
4. Check under **Model lanes** that every lane shows a PHOENIQS model and meets the rule.

PHOENIQS bills you directly. The box does not meter it and does not show its cost per run.

## Model lanes

A [model lane](/start/glossary/#model-lane) decides which model a worker uses. Each worker has one lane permission. A worker without one runs in the fallback lane. The panel **Model lanes** is on **Administration**, sub-tab **Packages**.

1. For **Cheap**, **Standard** and **Frontier**, choose a model from the list. For a model that is not listed, choose **Other model...** and type its id.
2. Choose the **Fallback lane (no lane permission)**.
3. Press **Save lanes**.
4. Press **Apply to installed packages**. The box names each worker it changed.
5. Read the table: **Lane**, **Model**, and whether it meets the data rule. Press **Check providers now** to check again.

"An automatic model is not allowed: choose a fixed model for the ... lane." The box refuses models that pick a provider by themselves, because the data rule could not be checked.

## What you see afterwards

- The header's status line shows the provider and model.
- Each run card shows its lane, its model and its data rule.
- A lane that does not meet the rule refuses its runs before any model call, with a message that names the lane.

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Save provider** | Switches the AI provider and resets the lanes to its defaults. | A new provider. | Yes. |
| **Save data rule** | Sets what providers may do and where. | Before the first task, and when your rules change. | Yes. |
| **Save lanes** | Sets the model per lane. | To use a cheaper or stronger model. | Yes. |
| **Apply to installed packages** | Rewrites installed workers to the saved lanes. | After every lane or provider change. | Yes: change and apply again. |
