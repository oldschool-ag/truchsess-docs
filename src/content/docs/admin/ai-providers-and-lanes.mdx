Everything on this page is on **Administration** › **Agent rules**: the panels **AI of this box**, **Model lanes** (with the button **Change AI provider**) and **Which AI providers may see your data**.

## Who can do this

The administrator. Changing the provider and switching to the managed AI work only in the office, on **https://myai.local/**. The data rule and the model lanes can also be changed on the remote address.

## Before you start

- To change the provider: the new provider's API key.
- To switch to the managed AI: the activation code from the card.
- Know the rule for your data: may a provider keep it or train on it, and where may it be processed?

## The AI of this box

Open **Administration**, then **Agent rules**. The panel **AI of this box** says which AI every agent uses:

- "Managed AI": the key came with the box, and only providers that never keep or train on your data are used.
- "Your own AI key": the key you entered under **Change AI provider**. The panel adds: "An activation code from the operator switches the box to the managed AI."

The provider and model are named in brackets.

## Switch to managed AI with an activation code

<NotYet>
Switching with an activation code needs the store's activation service, which is not available yet. Until then, use [Change AI provider](#change-the-ai-provider) with your own key, or ask support.
</NotYet>

The code from the card enrols the box in the store, if it is not enrolled yet, and delivers the AI key with a starter credit (see [Budget](/admin/budget/#the-starter-credit)). Your own key is replaced. You can go back to it at any time with **Change AI provider**.

1. Open **Administration**, then **Agent rules**, panel **AI of this box**.
2. Under **Switch to managed AI with an activation code**, enter the **Activation code** from the card.
3. Press **Switch to managed AI**.
4. Follow the steps: "Enrol this box in the store", "Store the AI key", "Set up the managed AI (never kept, never trained on)", "Set the starter credit", "Test the AI".
5. When all steps are done, the panel shows "AI is ready."

If a step fails, the panel says why in one sentence, and **Try again** appears. **Try again** starts again at the step that failed. The code is needed only until the store has accepted it.

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "This activation code was already used. Ask the operator for a new one." | Ask support for a new code. |
| "The store does not know this activation code. Check the code on the card and press Try again." | Check the code and press **Try again**. |
| "The store could not be reached. Check that the box is connected to the internet, then press Try again. The code was not used." | Check the internet connection and press **Try again**. |
| "This store does not deliver the AI to a box that is already enrolled yet. The box stays as it is; use your own AI key, or ask the operator." | Nothing changed. Keep your own key, or ask support. |
| "The box is enrolled in the store, but this activation code carries no AI key. Use your own AI key instead, or ask the operator." | Use **Change AI provider** with your own key, or ask support. |
| "The provider refused the AI key. Ask the operator, or use your own AI key." | Ask support, or use **Change AI provider** with your own key. |

## Change the AI provider

1. Open **Administration**, then **Agent rules**.
2. In the head of the panel **Model lanes**, press **Change AI provider**. The page **Choose the appliance AI** opens.
3. Choose the **Billing mode**, the **Provider** and the **Model ID**, and enter the key.
4. Press **Save provider**. The box restarts its AI part: "Starting the assistant".
5. The model lanes now hold the new provider's default models. Go to [Model lanes](#model-lanes) and press **Apply to installed agents**.

A key you save here replaces the managed AI and ends a starter credit. **Cancel** takes you back to **Agent rules** without a change.

The choices are explained on [AI providers](/setup/ai-providers/#your-choices).

## The data rule

The panel **Which AI providers may see your data** is on **Administration** › **Agent rules**. "Every model request of every agent goes only to providers that meet this rule." A run that cannot meet it is refused before any data leaves the box.

1. Under **What providers may do with your data**, choose one option.
2. Under **Where your data may be processed**, choose one option.
3. Press **Save data rule**.

| What providers may do | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Never kept, never trained on** (default) | "Only providers that neither keep your data nor train on it (zero data retention)." |
| **Never trained on** | "Only providers that never train on your data. They may keep it for a while, for example for abuse monitoring." |
| **Any provider, may keep and train on your data** | "Any provider. Some keep your data and train their models on it." |

| Where | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Any country** (default) | Providers in any country. |
| **EU or Switzerland** | Only PHOENIQS (Switzerland) or a model on this box. OpenRouter lanes are refused. |
| **Switzerland only** | Only PHOENIQS (Switzerland) or a model on this box. Every other lane is refused before any call. |
| **On this box only** | Only a model running on the box. "No local model ships yet: runs are refused." |

Agents that read or write your code or knowledge, or use stored credentials, always run at least at **Never trained on**, whatever you choose.

The managed AI runs only under **Never kept, never trained on**. With another rule, the step "Set up the managed AI (never kept, never trained on)" stops and says so.

## The Swiss-only option

To keep your data in Switzerland:

1. Get an API key from PHOENIQS.
2. Change the provider to **PHOENIQS (Switzerland)** with **Billing mode** **Own key** (see above).
3. Set the data rule's **Where your data may be processed** to **Switzerland only**, and save.
4. Check under **Model lanes** that every lane shows a PHOENIQS model and meets the rule.

PHOENIQS bills you directly. The box does not meter it and does not show its cost per run.

## Model lanes

A [model lane](/start/glossary/#model-lane) decides which model an agent uses. Each agent has one lane permission. An agent without one runs in the fallback lane. The panel **Model lanes** is on **Administration** › **Agent rules**.

1. For **Cheap**, **Standard** and **Frontier**, choose a model from the list. For a model that is not listed, choose **Other model...** and type its id.
2. Choose the **Fallback lane (no lane permission)**.
3. Press **Save lanes**.
4. Press **Apply to installed agents**. The box names each agent it changed.
5. Read the table: **Lane**, **Model**, and whether it meets the data rule. Press **Check providers now** to check again.

"An automatic model is not allowed: choose a fixed model for the ... lane." The box refuses models that pick a provider by themselves, because the data rule could not be checked.

## What you see afterwards

- The status line at the bottom of the navigation shows the provider and model.
- The panel **AI of this box** says whether the box uses the managed AI or your own key.
- Each run card shows its lane, its model and its data rule.
- A lane that does not meet the rule refuses its runs before any model call, with a message that names the lane.

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Switch to managed AI** | Uses the AI key from the activation code, with a starter credit. Your own key is replaced. | You have an activation code and want the managed AI. | Yes: **Change AI provider** with your own key. |
| **Save provider** | Switches the AI provider and resets the lanes to its defaults. | A new provider. | Yes. |
| **Save data rule** | Sets what providers may do and where. | Before the first task, and when your rules change. | Yes. |
| **Save lanes** | Sets the model per lane. | To use a cheaper or stronger model. | Yes. |
| **Apply to installed agents** | Rewrites installed agents to the saved lanes. | After every lane or provider change. | Yes: change and apply again. |

There is no model inside the box yet, so "On this box only" refuses every run. The box uses one AI provider at a time. You cannot set a different provider per agent.
</NotYet>
