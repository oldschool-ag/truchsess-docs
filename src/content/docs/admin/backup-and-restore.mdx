Safety copies (backups) are on **Settings** › **Updates and safety copies**, section **Safety copies**. The history of copies, key changes and restores is for experts: **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Box**, panel **Version and log**, **Backup history**. Advanced is the link **Advanced (for experts)** at the bottom of Settings.

## Who can do this

The administrator, in the office on **https://truchsess.local/**. Safety copies cannot be made, downloaded or restored from the remote address: "For safety, the recovery key, backups and restores work only in the office."

## What you need

- The recovery sheet (its key) that was current when the copy was made.
- For a restore: a moment when nobody works on the box. Everyone is signed out afterwards.

## The recovery sheet

The box makes no safety copy until a recovery sheet exists: "No recovery sheet yet: the box makes no safety copy until it exists."

1. Open **Settings** › **Updates and safety copies**, section **Safety copies**, part **Recovery sheet**.
2. Press **Make the recovery sheet**.
3. Save the key: **Copy**, or **Download as a file**. "The box shows this only once. Save it now, away from the box (a password manager, or printed and locked away)."
4. Press **I have saved it**.

The box keeps only the key's public half.

**Make a new recovery sheet** replaces the key. Press it, then **Confirm new key**: "Backups made before it can then only be opened with the old key; keep the old key as long as you keep those backups." The column **Recovery sheet** of **Copies on this box** shows which sheet each copy needs: "this sheet" or "an earlier sheet".

## Nightly safety copies

- Every night at 03:30 in your company's time zone (**Settings** › **Your company**, **Time zone**), the box makes a locked (encrypted) safety copy.
- Nightly and manual copies together: the newest 7 are kept.
- In a copy: tasks and their history, people (their passwords only in protected form), installed agents and their answers, settings, the owner policy, the agents' workspaces, the SSH keys.
- Never in a copy: the AI provider key, connection secrets (GitHub, API keys), the store enrolment, the remote access code.
- Tasks that are running when a copy starts are waited for up to 5 minutes. After that, the copy notes that their unfinished files may be incomplete.

**Back up now** (at the top of **Safety copies**) makes a copy at once, for example before an update.

## The copy in France

Once your storage in France is set up, the box also sends each night's copy there, locked with the same recovery sheet. Until then nothing leaves the box: the part **Copy in France** says "Not set up yet: the copy outside the box starts once your storage in France is set up."

After the first copy there, the part says, for example, "Last safety copy: today, tested on 3 November" and "Stored encrypted at our data centre in France". About once a month the box opens the newest copy in France to test it.

- Only your recovery sheet opens these copies: "without it nobody, Truchsess included, can open the copies."
- If the copy could not be sent for two nights, the part says "Your safety copy could not be sent for two nights. Your data is still on the box."
- If the monthly test did not pass: "The monthly test of your safety copy did not pass. Your data is still on the box."
- If the space is nearly used up: "The space for your safety copies is almost full. Old copies are thinned on their own; ask us for more space if this stays."

## Download a safety copy

In **Copies on this box**, press **Download** on a copy. The progress shows under the link, in the form "Downloading: ... of ... (...%)". The browser saves the file when it is complete. The file is called `truchsess-backup-` plus the time, and ends in `.age`. It is encrypted with the recovery sheet's key. Keep it outside the box.

If the connection breaks, the line says "The download broke ..." and nothing is saved. Press **Download** again.

## Restore on the same box

<Aside type="caution">
"A restore puts the box back to the copy's state: tasks, agents, people and settings made after it are gone, and everyone signs in again."
</Aside>

1. On **Settings** › **Updates and safety copies**, section **Safety copies**, part **Restore**, choose the **Safety copy**: one of the box's copies, the copy in France (for example **The copy in France from 3 November, 03:30**) once your storage there is set up, or **A backup file from this computer** and then the **Safety copy file**.
2. Paste **The key from your recovery sheet**, or choose it under **or the recovery sheet file**.
3. Press **Restore**, then **Confirm restore** within 10 seconds.
4. Follow the steps: "Open the backup with the recovery key", "Check the release and every file", "Keep a safety copy of the box", "Stop the services", "Put the backup in place", "Update the database to this release", "Start the services", "Check that everything runs".
5. The box signs everyone out: "The restore has finished its work and signed everyone out." Sign in again with an account from the copy.
6. Read the table **Enter these again** and enter each secret where it says.

If a step fails, the box returns to how it was before.

## Restore on a new box

Use this when you move to a new box or replace a broken one.

1. Install the new box. See [Unbox and connect](/setup/unbox-and-connect/).
2. Open **https://truchsess.local/**. On "Welcome to your Truchsess box", press **I am moving from another Truchsess box**. Do not create an account first.
3. If the page asks for it, enter the code (**Code from the card**, or **Code from the installer**).
4. Choose the **Backup file**. If the new box already has your storage in France, you can tick **Use the newest copy in France instead of a file** instead.
5. Paste the **Recovery key**, or choose it under **or the recovery key file**.
6. Press **Restore**.
7. When it is done, read **Enter these again** and press **Sign in**. Sign in with an account from the copy.

On a box that is already set up this is refused. Restore there under **Settings** › **Updates and safety copies** instead.

## What you see afterwards

The table **Enter these again** lists only what this box does not hold. Enter each one here:

| What | Where to enter it |
| --- | --- |
| The AI provider key | **Settings** › **Advanced** › **AI**, panel **Own AI key**: **Change AI provider**. With the managed AI, use the panel **AI of this box**, **Switch to managed AI with an activation code** (see [AI providers and lanes](/admin/ai-providers-and-lanes/#switch-to-managed-ai-with-an-activation-code)). |
| GitHub | **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Connections**, panel **GitHub**: **Connect GitHub** |
| The API key for a service | **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Connections**, panel **API keys** |
| The store enrolment | **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Agents**, **Store connection**: **Enrol** |
| Remote access (it is switched off) | **Settings** › **People and access**, **Reach the box from home** |

"Nothing: this box still holds every password and key the copy's box had." means there is nothing to enter.

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Back up now** | A safety copy at once. | Before big changes. | n/a |
| **Download** | Saves a copy as a file. | Regularly. | n/a |
| **Restore** (same box) | Puts the box back to the copy. | After a mistake you cannot fix otherwise. | No. Make a copy first, so you can restore that one. |
| **I am moving from another Truchsess box** (new box) | Moves a box's work to a new box. | New or replacement box. | No. |

## If it does not work

- "This recovery key does not open this backup. Use the recovery key that was current when the backup was made. Nothing was changed."
- "This backup comes from a newer release (...). Update this box first, then restore."
- "The disk is full: the restore stopped and the box was put back. Free some space and try again."
