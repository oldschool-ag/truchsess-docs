On a box where the operator switched it on, the agents can run on a ChatGPT plan instead of the box's AI key. You sign in to ChatGPT once, and the box moves its three model lanes to ChatGPT models after a short test. Tasks on ChatGPT are not charged to the box's budget: they count against the plan's own limits.

The ChatGPT sign-in is off on every box. Only the operator can switch it on. When it is off, the section described here is not on the page.

## Who can do this

The administrator, in the office, on **https://truchsess.local/**.

## What you need

- A box where the operator switched the ChatGPT sign-in on.
- A ChatGPT plan: Business, Enterprise or Edu, or a personal Plus or Pro plan.
- For Plus or Pro: in ChatGPT, under Settings, Data controls, "Improve the model for everyone" is switched off. The box cannot check this setting; you confirm it.
- The data rule set to **Never trained on**. Read [The data rule decides](#the-data-rule-decides) first.
- A phone or computer where you can sign in to ChatGPT.

## The data rule decides

ChatGPT keeps conversations for a while. So ChatGPT never meets the rule **Never kept, never trained on**, the box's default. It meets **Never trained on**: for Business, Enterprise and Edu always, for Plus and Pro once you confirm that training is switched off. This is your choice, with a consequence either way:

| Your data rule | What it means for ChatGPT | The consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keep **Never kept, never trained on** | ChatGPT cannot be used. **Use ChatGPT on all lanes** answers "ChatGPT sign-in is not 'never kept'. To use it on this box, set the data rule to 'never trained on'." and nothing changes. | No provider may keep your data. The agents stay on the box's AI key, and their tasks are charged to the box's budget. |
| Change to **Never trained on** | ChatGPT can be used. | ChatGPT, and every other provider, may keep your data for a while, for example to watch for abuse. No provider may train on it. |

The data rule is the panel **Which AI providers may see your data** under **Settings** › **Advanced** › **Agent rules**. See [The data rule](/admin/ai-providers-and-lanes/#the-data-rule). Agents that read your code, knowledge, mail or credentials always run at least at **Never trained on**, whatever you choose.

## Steps

The section is on the page **The AI for your box**: open **Settings** › **Advanced** › **AI** (the link **Advanced (for experts)** at the bottom of **Settings**), and in the panel **Own AI key** press **Change AI provider**. Scroll to "Sign in with ChatGPT (operator only)".

### Sign in

1. Press **Sign in with ChatGPT**. The page says "Asking OpenAI for a sign-in code…".
2. The page shows a link to OpenAI's page and a code: "Open ... on any device, sign in to ChatGPT there and enter this code:". Under it: when the code expires.
3. Open the link on any phone or computer, sign in to ChatGPT there, and enter the code.
4. Wait. The box never sees your ChatGPT password. When the sign-in is done, the page says "Signed in as ..." with the e-mail address, the plan, and how much of the plan's usage windows is used.

### Confirm training is off (Plus or Pro only)

For a personal plan, the page says "This is a personal ChatGPT plan (...). OpenAI may train on it unless training is switched off in ChatGPT, Settings, Data controls. The box cannot check this."

1. In ChatGPT, under Settings, Data controls, switch off "Improve the model for everyone".
2. On the box, tick "I switched off 'Improve the model for everyone' for ..." with your address.
3. Press **Confirm**.

Business, Enterprise and Edu need nothing more.

### Move the lanes to ChatGPT

1. Set the data rule to **Never trained on** and press **Save data rule** (see [The data rule decides](#the-data-rule-decides)).
2. Back on **The AI for your box**, press **Use ChatGPT on all lanes**.
3. The page says "Testing ChatGPT with one short message per lane…". The box sends ChatGPT one short test message for each lane. This can take a few minutes.
4. When ChatGPT answered for every lane, the page says "Lanes set to the ChatGPT subscription. Apply them to installed packages to rewrite their models."
5. Open **Settings** › **Advanced** › **AI**, panel **Model lanes**, and press **Apply to installed agents**. See [Model lanes](/admin/ai-providers-and-lanes/#model-lanes).

If ChatGPT does not answer, the lanes do not change: the previous AI stays, and the page says why in one line (see below).

### One agent on ChatGPT

You can also put a single agent on a model of your ChatGPT plan, with its reasoning, and leave the lanes as they are: in the agent's settings, **For administrators**, **AI for this agent**. See [Choose an agent's AI](/admin/agent-ai/).

## What you see afterwards

- The lanes **Cheap**, **Standard** and **Frontier** hold ChatGPT models, each one that answered the test.
- **Settings** › **Advanced** › **AI**, panel **Costs in detail**, says "ChatGPT subscription, not metered: plan ...", and "Lanes on ChatGPT models count against the plan's own limits, not this budget."
- Tasks on ChatGPT are not charged to the box's budget. The test messages are not charged either; they count against the plan's limits like every ChatGPT message.
- When the plan's limit is reached, a task stops with "Your ChatGPT plan's usage limit is reached. It resets at ..." with the time.
- When the sign-in expires, a task stops with "The ChatGPT sign-in has expired. Sign in with ChatGPT again under Settings > Advanced > AI." Sign in again in the same section.
- In both cases sending the task again does not help: wait, or sign in again. See [When a task fails](/use/follow-a-run/#when-a-task-fails).

## If it does not work

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| An agent's card: "Problem: its AI is not available: the ChatGPT plan is signed out" | Sign in with ChatGPT again, or choose another AI for that agent. See [Choose an agent's AI](/admin/agent-ai/#if-it-does-not-work). |
| "ChatGPT sign-in is not 'never kept'. To use it on this box, set the data rule to 'never trained on'." | The data rule is **Never kept, never trained on**. Change it to **Never trained on** if you accept the consequence, then press **Use ChatGPT on all lanes** again. |
| "ChatGPT sign-in cannot serve ...: a personal ChatGPT plan may be used for training..." | Switch off training in ChatGPT and confirm it on the page (see [Confirm training is off](#confirm-training-is-off-plus-or-pro-only)). |
| "Waiting for the code to be entered at OpenAI… If OpenAI rejects the code, switch on 'Device code login' in ChatGPT, Settings, Security." | Enter the code at OpenAI. If OpenAI rejects it, switch on "Device code login" in ChatGPT under Settings, Security, then press **Sign in with ChatGPT** again. |
| "The sign-in did not finish." | Press **Sign in with ChatGPT** again. |
| "ChatGPT answered: this model is not in your plan. Choose a ChatGPT model under Settings > Advanced > AI." | The plan offers no model that answered for a lane. Keep the previous AI, or choose a ChatGPT model under **Model lanes**. |
| "Your ChatGPT plan's usage limit is reached. Try again when it resets." | Wait until the plan's limit resets, then press **Use ChatGPT on all lanes** again. |
| "The ChatGPT sign-in has expired. Sign in with ChatGPT again under Settings > Advanced > AI." | Press **Sign in with ChatGPT** and sign in again. |
| "The box cannot reach ChatGPT." | Check that the box reaches the internet, then try again. |
| "ChatGPT did not answer the test: ..." | Try again later. The previous AI stays. |
| "The test did not finish. The previous AI stays." | Press **Use ChatGPT on all lanes** again. |
| "The test is out of date. Press the button again." | Press **Use ChatGPT on all lanes** again. |
| "Sign in with ChatGPT first." | Sign in, then press the button. |
| The section "Sign in with ChatGPT (operator only)" is not on the page. | The ChatGPT sign-in is off on this box. Only the operator can switch it on. |

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does | When to pick it | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Sign in with ChatGPT** | Connects the box to your ChatGPT plan. Nothing changes for the agents yet. | Before you use ChatGPT. | Yes: **Sign out**. |
| **Confirm** (Plus or Pro) | Records that training is switched off in ChatGPT. | Only after you switched it off. | Sign out ends it. |
| **Use ChatGPT on all lanes** | Tests ChatGPT once per lane, then moves all three lanes to ChatGPT models. | When you want the agents to run on the plan. | Yes: choose other models under **Model lanes**, or **Change AI provider**. |
| **Refresh** | Reads the plan and how much of its limits is used. | To see when a limit resets. | n/a |
| **Sign out** | Ends the sign-in. The lanes keep their ChatGPT models, and their tasks fail until you sign in again or choose other models under **Model lanes**. | When you stop using ChatGPT. | Sign in again. |

You cannot switch the ChatGPT sign-in on yourself. Use the box's AI key or your own AI key under **Change AI provider** (see [AI providers and lanes](/admin/ai-providers-and-lanes/)), or ask support.
